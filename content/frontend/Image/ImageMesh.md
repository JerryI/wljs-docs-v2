---
title: ImageMesh
---

`ImageMesh[image]` returns the foreground region in image as a `BoundaryMeshRegion` object.

## Examples

```wolfram
ImageMesh[Binarize[ExampleData[{"TestImage", "Lena"}]]]
(* BoundaryMeshRegion[...] *)
```
