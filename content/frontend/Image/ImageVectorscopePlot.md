---
title: ImageVectorscopePlot
---

`ImageVectorscopePlot[image]` plots the chrominance of image.

## Examples

Plot the vectorscope of an image:

```wolfram
ImageVectorscopePlot[ExampleData[{"TestImage", "Lena"}]]
```
