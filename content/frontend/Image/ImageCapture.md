---
title: ImageCapture
---

`ImageCapture[]` opens a graphical user interface for capturing images from connected cameras.

## Examples

Open the image capture interface:

```wolfram
ImageCapture[]
```

Capture with a specific device:

```wolfram
ImageCapture["FaceTime HD Camera"]
```
