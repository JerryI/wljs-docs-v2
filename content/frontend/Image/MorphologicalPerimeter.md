---
title: MorphologicalPerimeter
---

`MorphologicalPerimeter[image]` picks out the morphological perimeter of regions of foreground in *image*.

- `MorphologicalPerimeter[image, t]` treats values above *t* as foreground.

## Examples

```wolfram
MorphologicalPerimeter[DiskMatrix[10]]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/MorphologicalPerimeter.html) for more details.*
