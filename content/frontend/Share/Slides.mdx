Any presentation made with [slide cells](./../Cell%20types/Slide) can be exported individually, preserving all graphics and styling in automatic mode to a PDF document. Call <small>Share</small> from the top menu or click the <span className='inline-flex' ><Share2 style={{height:"1rem"}}/></span> icon and choose <small>Slides</small>:

## How to Use
Evaluate your aggregation slides cell [(see Merging All Slides section)](./../Cell%20types/Slide):

<WLJSWrapper>
<span className="absolute -inset-1 bg-gradient-to-r from-fd-primary/50 to-purple-500/50 rounded-lg blur-xl opacity-15 group-hover:opacity-50 transition duration-500" />

`}</wljs-editor>

Run the __export option__.

Presentation slides are recorded and converted to a PDF document live and automatically.
