---
title: Embed Notebook Files
---

This feature allows you to embed some files or folders into the notebook and create a self-extracting notebook.

Call <small>Embed files</small> from the top-level menu:

<div className='relative group'>
<div className="absolute -inset-0.5 bg-gradient-to-r from-fd-primary/50 to-purple-500/50 rounded-lg blur blur-3xl opacity-5 group-hover:opacity-10 transition duration-500" />

![Embed](./imgs/embed-feature.png)

</div>


<Callout>
This does not export anything to a new file — it modifies your current notebook by embedding the binary compressed data. Don't forget to save the notebook afterward.
</Callout>

Upon the next fresh launch, the data is automatically unpacked and the archive is removed from the notebook.
