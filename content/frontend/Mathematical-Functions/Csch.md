---
title: Csch
---

`Csch[z]` gives the hyperbolic cosecant of z.

## Examples

Hyperbolic cosecant values:

```wolfram
Csch[1.0]
(* 0.850918 *)
```

Symbolic evaluation:

```wolfram
Csch[Log[2]]
(* 4/3 *)
```
