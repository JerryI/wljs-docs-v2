---
title: SixJSymbol
---

`SixJSymbol[{j1, j2, j3}, {j4, j5, j6}]` gives the values of the Wigner 6-j symbol.

## Examples

```wolfram
SixJSymbol[{1, 1, 1}, {1, 1, 1}]
(* 1/6 *)
```

```wolfram
SixJSymbol[{2, 2, 2}, {2, 2, 2}]
(* 1/14 *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/SixJSymbol.html) for more details.
