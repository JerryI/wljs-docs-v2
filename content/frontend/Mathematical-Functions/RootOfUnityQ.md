---
title: RootOfUnityQ
---

`RootOfUnityQ[a]` yields True if a is a root of unity, and yields False otherwise.

## Examples

```wolfram
RootOfUnityQ[Exp[2 Pi I/5]]
```

```wolfram
RootOfUnityQ[Sqrt[2]]
```
