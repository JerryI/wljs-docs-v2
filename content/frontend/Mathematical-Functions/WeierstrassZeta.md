---
title: WeierstrassZeta
---

`WeierstrassZeta[u,{g2,g3}]` gives the Weierstrass zeta function ζ(u;g2,g3).

## Examples

Evaluate the Weierstrass zeta function:

```wolfram
WeierstrassZeta[0.5, {1, 2}]
(* 1.94773 *)
```
