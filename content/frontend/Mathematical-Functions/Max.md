---
title: Max
---

`Max[x1, x2, ...]` yields the numerically largest of the xi.

`Max[{x1, x2, ...}, {y1, ...}, ...]` yields the largest element of any of the lists.

## Examples

Maximum of numbers:

```wolfram
Max[3, 1, 4, 1, 5, 9]
(* 9 *)
```

Maximum of a list:

```wolfram
Max[{-5, 3, 7, -2}]
(* 7 *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Max.html) for more details.
