---
title: LCM
---

`LCM[n1, n2, ...]` gives the least common multiple of the integers.

## Examples

Find LCM of two numbers:

```wolfram
LCM[4, 6]
(* 12 *)
```

LCM of multiple numbers:

```wolfram
LCM[3, 4, 5]
(* 60 *)
```

LCM of coprime numbers:

```wolfram
LCM[7, 11]
(* 77 *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/LCM.html) for more details.*
