---
title: Gudermannian
---

`Gudermannian[z]` gives the Gudermannian function gd(z).

## Examples

Compute the Gudermannian of a value:

```wolfram
Gudermannian[1.0]
(* 0.865769 *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Gudermannian.html) for more details.
