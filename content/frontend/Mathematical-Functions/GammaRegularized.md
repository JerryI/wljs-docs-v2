---
title: GammaRegularized
---

`GammaRegularized[a, z]` is the regularized incomplete gamma function Q(a,z).

## Examples

Compute regularized gamma:

```wolfram
GammaRegularized[2, 1]
(* 2/E *)
```

Numerical value:

```wolfram
N[GammaRegularized[3, 2]]
(* 0.676676 *)
```
