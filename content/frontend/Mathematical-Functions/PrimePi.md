---
title: PrimePi
---

`PrimePi[x]` gives the number of primes π(x) less than or equal to x.

## Examples

Count primes up to 100:

```wolfram
PrimePi[100]
(* 25 *)
```

Count primes up to 1000:

```wolfram
PrimePi[1000]
(* 168 *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/PrimePi.html) for more details.*
