---
title: LogSeriesDistribution
---

`LogSeriesDistribution[θ]` represents a logarithmic series distribution with parameter θ.

## Examples

```wolfram
dist = LogSeriesDistribution[0.5];
PDF[dist, k]
```

```wolfram
RandomVariate[LogSeriesDistribution[0.8], 10]
```
