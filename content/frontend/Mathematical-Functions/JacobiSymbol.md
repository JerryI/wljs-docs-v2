---
title: JacobiSymbol
---

`JacobiSymbol[n, m]` gives the Jacobi symbol (n/m).

## Examples

Compute Jacobi symbol:

```wolfram
JacobiSymbol[3, 7]
(* -1 *)
```

For prime modulus (Legendre symbol):

```wolfram
JacobiSymbol[2, 11]
(* -1 *)
```
