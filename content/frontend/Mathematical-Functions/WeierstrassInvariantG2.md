---
title: WeierstrassInvariantG2
---

`WeierstrassInvariantG2[{ω, ω′}]` gives the invariant g2 for the Weierstrass elliptic functions corresponding to the half-periods {ω, ω′}.

## Examples

```wolfram
WeierstrassInvariantG2[{1, I}]
```

```wolfram
WeierstrassInvariantG2[{1/2, 1/2 + I/2}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/WeierstrassInvariantG2.html) for more details.*
