---
title: JacobiCD
---

`JacobiCD[u,m]` gives the Jacobi elliptic function cd(u|m).

## Examples

Evaluate the Jacobi CD function:

```wolfram
JacobiCD[0.5, 0.3]
(* 0.938791 *)
```

Plot the function:

```wolfram
Plot[JacobiCD[x, 0.5], {x, 0, 4}]
```
