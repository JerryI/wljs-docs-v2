---
title: HankelH2
---

`HankelH2[n, z]` gives the Hankel function of the second kind $H_n^{(2)}(z)$.

## Examples

Compute Hankel H2:

```wolfram
HankelH2[0, 1.0]
(* 0.765198 - 0.088257 I *)
```

Plot the real part:

```wolfram
Plot[Re[HankelH2[0, x]], {x, 0.1, 10}]
```
