---
title: WeierstrassHalfPeriodW2
---

`WeierstrassHalfPeriodW2[{g2,g3}]` gives the half-period ω2 for the Weierstrass elliptic functions corresponding to the invariants {g2,g3}.

## Examples

Compute the half-period ω2:

```wolfram
WeierstrassHalfPeriodW2[{1, 2}]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/WeierstrassHalfPeriodW2.html) for more details.
