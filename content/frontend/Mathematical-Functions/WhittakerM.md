---
title: WhittakerM
---

`WhittakerM[k,m,z]` gives the Whittaker function $M_{k,m}(z)$.

## Examples

Evaluate the Whittaker M function:

```wolfram
WhittakerM[1, 0.5, 2.0]
(* 2.71828 *)
```

Plot the function:

```wolfram
Plot[WhittakerM[0.5, 0.25, x], {x, 0, 5}]
```
