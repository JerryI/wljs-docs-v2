---
title: LogisticSigmoid
---

`LogisticSigmoid[z]` gives the logistic sigmoid function.

## Examples

```wolfram
LogisticSigmoid[0]
```

```wolfram
Plot[LogisticSigmoid[x], {x, -5, 5}]
```
