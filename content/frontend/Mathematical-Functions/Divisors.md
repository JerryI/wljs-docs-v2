---
title: Divisors
---

`Divisors[n]` gives a list of the integers that divide n.

## Examples

Find all divisors:

```wolfram
Divisors[24]
(* {1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24} *)
```

Divisors of a prime:

```wolfram
Divisors[17]
(* {1, 17} *)
```

Count divisors:

```wolfram
Length[Divisors[100]]
(* 9 *)
```
