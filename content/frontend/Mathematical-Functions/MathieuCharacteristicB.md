---
title: MathieuCharacteristicB
---

`MathieuCharacteristicB[r, q]` gives the characteristic value br for odd Mathieu functions with characteristic exponent r and parameter q.

## Examples

```wolfram
MathieuCharacteristicB[1, 0.5]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/MathieuCharacteristicB.html) for more details.*
