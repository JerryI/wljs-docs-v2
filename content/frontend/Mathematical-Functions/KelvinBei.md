---
title: KelvinBei
---

`KelvinBei[z]` gives the Kelvin function bei(z).

`KelvinBei[n, z]` gives the Kelvin function bei_n(z).

## Examples

Compute Kelvin bei:

```wolfram
KelvinBei[2.0]
(* 0.249218 *)
```

Plot:

```wolfram
Plot[KelvinBei[x], {x, 0, 10}]
```
