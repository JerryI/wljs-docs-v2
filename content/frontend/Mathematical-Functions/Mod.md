---
title: Mod
---

`Mod[m, n]` gives the remainder on division of m by n.

## Examples

Modulo operation:

```wolfram
Mod[17, 5]
(* 2 *)
```

```wolfram
Mod[10, 3]
(* 1 *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Mod.html) for more details.*
