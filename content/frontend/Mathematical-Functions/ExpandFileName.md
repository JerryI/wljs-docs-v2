---
title: ExpandFileName
---

`ExpandFileName["name"]` expands name to an absolute file path for your OS.

## Examples

Expand home directory:

```wolfram
ExpandFileName["~/Documents"]
(* "/Users/username/Documents" *)
```

Expand relative path:

```wolfram
ExpandFileName["./data"]
```
