---
title: BetaRegularized
---

`BetaRegularized[z, a, b]` gives the regularized incomplete beta function Iz(a,b).

## Examples

Compute regularized beta:

```wolfram
BetaRegularized[0.5, 2, 3]
(* 0.6875 *)
```

Symbolic value:

```wolfram
BetaRegularized[1/2, 1, 1]
(* 1/2 *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/BetaRegularized.html) for more details.
