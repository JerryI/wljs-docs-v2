---
title: CarlsonRC
---

`CarlsonRC[x, y]` gives Carlson's elliptic integral RC(x, y).

## Examples

Evaluate Carlson RC:

```wolfram
N[CarlsonRC[1, 2]]
(* 0.785398 *)
```

Symbolic form:

```wolfram
CarlsonRC[0, 1]
(* Pi/2 *)
```
