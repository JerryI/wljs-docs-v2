---
title: CosIntegral
---

`CosIntegral[z]` gives the cosine integral function Ci(z).

## Examples

```wolfram
CosIntegral[1.0]
(* 0.337404 *)
```

```wolfram
N[CosIntegral[2]]
(* 0.422981 *)
```
