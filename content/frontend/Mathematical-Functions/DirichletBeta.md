---
title: DirichletBeta
---

`DirichletBeta[s]` gives the Dirichlet beta function β(s).

## Examples

Evaluate at specific points:

```wolfram
DirichletBeta[2]
(* Catalan *)
```

Numerical value:

```wolfram
N[DirichletBeta[3]]
(* 0.968946 *)
```
