---
title: Ceiling
---

`Ceiling[x]` gives the smallest integer greater than or equal to x.

`Ceiling[x, a]` gives the smallest multiple of a greater than or equal to x.

## Examples

Ceiling of a number:

```wolfram
Ceiling[3.2]
(* 4 *)

Ceiling[-2.7]
(* -2 *)
```

Ceiling to a multiple:

```wolfram
Ceiling[17, 5]
(* 20 *)
```
