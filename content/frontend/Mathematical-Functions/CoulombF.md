---
title: CoulombF
---

`CoulombF[l, η, r]` gives the regular Coulomb wavefunction Fl(η, r).

## Examples

Compute regular Coulomb wavefunction:

```wolfram
CoulombF[0, 1, 2.0]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/CoulombF.html) for more details.*
