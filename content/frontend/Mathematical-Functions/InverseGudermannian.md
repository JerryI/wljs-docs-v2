---
title: InverseGudermannian
---

`InverseGudermannian[z]` gives the inverse Gudermannian function gd⁻¹(z).

## Examples

Compute the inverse Gudermannian:
```wolfram
InverseGudermannian[Pi/4]
```

Verify the inverse:
```wolfram
Gudermannian[InverseGudermannian[0.5]]
```
