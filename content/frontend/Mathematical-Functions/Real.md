---
title: Real
---

`Real` is the head used for real (floating-point) numbers.

## Examples

Check head of a real number:

```wolfram
Head[3.14]
(* Real *)
```

Pattern matching:

```wolfram
MatchQ[2.718, _Real]
(* True *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Real.html) for more details.*
