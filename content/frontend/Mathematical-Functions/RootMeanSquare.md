---
title: RootMeanSquare
---

`RootMeanSquare[list]` gives the root mean square of values in list.

`RootMeanSquare[dist]` gives the root mean square of the distribution dist.

## Examples

```wolfram
RootMeanSquare[{1, 2, 3, 4, 5}]
(* Sqrt[11] *)
```

```wolfram
RootMeanSquare[{-2, 2, -2, 2}]
(* 2 *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/RootMeanSquare.html) for more details.
