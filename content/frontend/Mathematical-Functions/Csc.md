---
title: Csc
---

`Csc[z]` gives the cosecant of z.

## Examples

Cosecant of common angles:

```wolfram
Csc[Pi/6]
(* 2 *)

Csc[Pi/4]
(* Sqrt[2] *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
Csc[1.0]
(* 1.1884 *)
```
