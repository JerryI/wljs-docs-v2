---
title: ArcCoth
---

`ArcCoth[z]` gives the inverse hyperbolic cotangent coth⁻¹(z) of the complex number z.

## Examples

Evaluate the inverse hyperbolic cotangent:

```wolfram
ArcCoth[2]
(* Log[3]/2 *)
```

Numerical value:

```wolfram
N[ArcCoth[2]]
(* 0.549306 *)
```
