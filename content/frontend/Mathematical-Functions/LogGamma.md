---
title: LogGamma
---

`LogGamma[z]` gives the logarithm of the gamma function log Γ(z).

## Examples

Compute log gamma:

```wolfram
LogGamma[5]
(* Log[24] *)
```

Numerical value:

```wolfram
LogGamma[10.0]
(* 12.8018 *)
```
