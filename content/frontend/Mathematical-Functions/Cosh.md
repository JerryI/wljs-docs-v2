---
title: Cosh
---

`Cosh[z]` gives the hyperbolic cosine of `z`, defined as $(e^z + e^{-z})/2$.

## Examples

Compute hyperbolic cosine values:

```wolfram
Cosh[0]
(* 1 *)

Cosh[1.0]
(* 1.54308 *)

Cosh[Log[2]]
(* 5/4 *)
```
