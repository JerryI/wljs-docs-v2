---
title: WeierstrassP
---

`WeierstrassP[u, {g2, g3}]` gives the Weierstrass elliptic function ℘(u; g2, g3).

## Examples

Evaluate Weierstrass P function:

```wolfram
WeierstrassP[0.5, {1, 2}]
(* 3.40975 *)
```

Symbolic form:

```wolfram
WeierstrassP[z, {g2, g3}]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/WeierstrassP.html) for more details.
