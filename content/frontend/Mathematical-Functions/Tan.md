---
title: Tan
---

`Tan[z]` gives the tangent of z.

## Examples

Tangent of an angle:

```wolfram
Tan[Pi/4]
(* 1 *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
N[Tan[1]]
(* 1.5574 *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Tan.html) for more details.
