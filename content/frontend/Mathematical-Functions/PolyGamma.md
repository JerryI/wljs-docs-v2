---
title: PolyGamma
---

`PolyGamma[z]` gives the digamma function ψ(z).

`PolyGamma[n, z]` gives the nth derivative of the digamma function ψ^(n)(z).

## Examples

```wolfram
PolyGamma[1]
(* -EulerGamma *)
```
