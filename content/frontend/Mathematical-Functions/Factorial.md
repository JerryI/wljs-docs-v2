---
title: Factorial
---

`n!` or `Factorial[n]` gives the factorial of n (the product 1×2×3×...×n).

## Examples

Compute factorials:

```wolfram
5!
(* 120 *)

10!
(* 3628800 *)
```

Factorial of zero:

```wolfram
0!
(* 1 *)
```

Large factorials:

```wolfram
100!
(* 93326215443944152681699238856266700490715968264381621468592963... *)
```
