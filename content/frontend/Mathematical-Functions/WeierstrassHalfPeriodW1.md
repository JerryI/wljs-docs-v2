---
title: WeierstrassHalfPeriodW1
---

`WeierstrassHalfPeriodW1[{g2, g3}]` gives the half-period ω1 for Weierstrass elliptic functions corresponding to the invariants {g2, g3}.

## Examples

```wolfram
WeierstrassHalfPeriodW1[{1, 2}]
```
