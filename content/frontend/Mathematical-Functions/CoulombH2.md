---
title: CoulombH2
---

`CoulombH2[l, η, r]` gives the incoming irregular Coulomb wavefunction Hl(-)(η, r).

## Examples

Compute incoming Coulomb wavefunction:

```wolfram
CoulombH2[0, 1, 2.0]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/CoulombH2.html) for more details.*
