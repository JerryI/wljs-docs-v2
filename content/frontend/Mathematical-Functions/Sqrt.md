---
title: Sqrt
---

`Sqrt[z]` gives the square root of z.

## Examples

Exact square roots:

```wolfram
Sqrt[16]
(* 4 *)

Sqrt[2]
(* Sqrt[2] - kept symbolic *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
Sqrt[2.0]
(* 1.41421 *)
```
