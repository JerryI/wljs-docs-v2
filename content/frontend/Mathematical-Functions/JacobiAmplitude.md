---
title: JacobiAmplitude
---

`JacobiAmplitude[u, m]` gives the amplitude am(u|m) for Jacobi elliptic functions.

## Examples

```wolfram
JacobiAmplitude[1, 0.5]
```

```wolfram
Plot[JacobiAmplitude[u, 0.8], {u, 0, 10}]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/JacobiAmplitude.html) for more details.
