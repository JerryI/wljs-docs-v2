---
title: JacobiDC
---

`JacobiDC[u, m]` gives the Jacobi elliptic function dc(u|m).

## Examples

Evaluate a Jacobi DC function:

```wolfram
JacobiDC[0.5, 0.3]
(* 1.03819 *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/JacobiDC.html) for more details.*
