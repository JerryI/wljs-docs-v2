---
title: HaarWavelet
---

`HaarWavelet[]` represents a Haar wavelet.

## Examples

Perform a discrete wavelet transform using Haar wavelet:

```wolfram
DiscreteWaveletTransform[{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8}, HaarWavelet[]]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/HaarWavelet.html) for more details.
