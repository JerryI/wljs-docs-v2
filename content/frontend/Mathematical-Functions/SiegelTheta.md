---
title: SiegelTheta
---

`SiegelTheta[Ω, s]` gives the Siegel theta function with Riemann modular matrix Ω and vector s.

`SiegelTheta[{ν1, ν2}, Ω, s]` gives the Siegel theta function with characteristics ν1 and ν2.

## Examples

Compute a Siegel theta function:

```wolfram
SiegelTheta[{{I}}, {0}]
(* 1.08643 *)
```
