---
title: MersennePrimeExponent
---

`MersennePrimeExponent[n]` gives the nth Mersenne prime exponent.

## Examples

First Mersenne prime exponent:

```wolfram
MersennePrimeExponent[1]
(* 2 *)
```

First few exponents:

```wolfram
Table[MersennePrimeExponent[n], {n, 1, 5}]
(* {2, 3, 5, 7, 13} *)
```
