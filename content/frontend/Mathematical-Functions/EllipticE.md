---
title: EllipticE
---

`EllipticE[m]` gives the complete elliptic integral E(m).

`EllipticE[ϕ, m]` gives the elliptic integral of the second kind E(ϕ|m).

## Examples

```wolfram
EllipticE[0.5]
(* 1.35064 *)
```

```wolfram
EllipticE[Pi/4, 0.5]
(* 0.748186 *)
```
