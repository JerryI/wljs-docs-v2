---
title: RamanujanTauL
---

`RamanujanTauL[s]` gives the Ramanujan tau Dirichlet L-function L(s).

## Examples

Evaluate the Ramanujan tau L-function:

```wolfram
RamanujanTauL[2] // N
(* 0.0393407 *)
```
