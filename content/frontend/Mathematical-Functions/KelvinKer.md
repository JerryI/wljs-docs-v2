---
title: KelvinKer
---

`KelvinKer[z]` gives the Kelvin function ker(z).

`KelvinKer[n, z]` gives the Kelvin function ker_n(z).

## Examples

Compute Kelvin ker:

```wolfram
KelvinKer[2.0]
(* -0.04166 *)
```

Plot:

```wolfram
Plot[KelvinKer[x], {x, 0, 10}]
```
