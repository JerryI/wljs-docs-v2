---
title: Fibonorial
---

`Fibonorial[n]` gives the fibonorial n!_F (product of the first n Fibonacci numbers).

## Examples

Compute the fibonorial of 5:

```wolfram
Fibonorial[5]
(* 30 *)
```

Compute the fibonorial of 10:

```wolfram
Fibonorial[10]
(* 122522400 *)
```
