---
title: OwenT
---

`OwenT[x, a]` gives Owen's T function T(x, a).

## Examples

Compute Owen's T function:
```wolfram
OwenT[1, 0.5]
```

Numerical evaluation:
```wolfram
N[OwenT[2, 1]]
```
