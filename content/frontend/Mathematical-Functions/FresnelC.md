---
title: FresnelC
---

`FresnelC[z]` gives the Fresnel cosine integral C(z).

## Examples

Compute Fresnel C:

```wolfram
FresnelC[1.0]
(* 0.779893 *)
```

Plot the function:

```wolfram
Plot[FresnelC[x], {x, 0, 5}]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/FresnelC.html) for more details.
