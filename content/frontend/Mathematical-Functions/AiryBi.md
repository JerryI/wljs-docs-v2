---
title: AiryBi
---

`AiryBi[z]` gives the Airy function Bi(z).

## Examples

Evaluate AiryBi at a point:

```wolfram
AiryBi[0]
(* 1/(3^(1/6) Gamma[2/3]) *)
```

Plot the Airy Bi function:

```wolfram
Plot[AiryBi[x], {x, -10, 2}]
```
