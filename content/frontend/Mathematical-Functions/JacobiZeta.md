---
title: JacobiZeta
---

`JacobiZeta[φ, m]` gives the Jacobi zeta function Z(φ|m).

## Examples

Compute Jacobi zeta:

```wolfram
JacobiZeta[Pi/4, 0.5]
(* 0.159213 *)
```

Plot the function:

```wolfram
Plot[JacobiZeta[x, 0.5], {x, 0, 2 Pi}]
```
