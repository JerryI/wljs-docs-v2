---
title: ScorerGi
---

`ScorerGi[z]` gives the Scorer function Gi(z).

## Examples

Evaluate the Scorer Gi function:
```wolfram
ScorerGi[1.5]
```

Plot the Scorer Gi function:
```wolfram
Plot[ScorerGi[x], {x, -10, 5}]
```
