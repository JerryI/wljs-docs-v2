---
title: Cos
---

`Cos[z]` gives the cosine of z.

## Examples

Cosine of common angles:

```wolfram
Cos[Pi/3]
(* 1/2 *)

Cos[Pi]
(* -1 *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
Cos[1.0]
(* 0.540302 *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Cos.html) for more details.
