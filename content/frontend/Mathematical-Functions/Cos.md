---
title: Cos
---

`Cos[z]` gives the cosine of z.

## Examples

Cosine of common angles:

```wolfram
Cos[Pi/3]
(* 1/2 *)

Cos[Pi]
(* -1 *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
Cos[1.0]
(* 0.540302 *)
```
