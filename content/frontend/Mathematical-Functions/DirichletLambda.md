---
title: DirichletLambda
---

`DirichletLambda[s]` gives the Dirichlet lambda function λ(s).

## Examples

Evaluate at specific points:

```wolfram
DirichletLambda[2]
(* Pi^2/8 *)
```

Numerical value:

```wolfram
N[DirichletLambda[4]]
(* 1.01467 *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/DirichletLambda.html) for more details.
