---
title: ClebschGordan
---

`ClebschGordan[{j1, m1}, {j2, m2}, {j, m}]` gives the Clebsch–Gordan coefficient for the decomposition of |j, m〉 in terms of |j1, m1〉|j2, m2〉.

## Examples

```wolfram
ClebschGordan[{1, 0}, {1, 0}, {0, 0}]
(* -1/Sqrt[3] *)
```

```wolfram
ClebschGordan[{1/2, 1/2}, {1/2, -1/2}, {1, 0}]
(* 1/Sqrt[2] *)
```
