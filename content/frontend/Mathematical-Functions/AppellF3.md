---
title: AppellF3
---

`AppellF3[a1, a2, b1, b2, c, x, y]` is the Appell hypergeometric function of two variables F3(a1, a2; b1, b2; c; x, y).

## Examples

Evaluate the Appell F3 function:

```wolfram
AppellF3[1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 0.3, 0.2]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/AppellF3.html) for more details.
