---
title: NevilleThetaN
---

`NevilleThetaN[z, m]` gives the Neville theta function θₙ(z|m).

## Examples

Compute Neville theta N:
```wolfram
NevilleThetaN[0.5, 0.3]
```

Plot the function:
```wolfram
Plot[NevilleThetaN[z, 0.5], {z, 0, 4}]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/NevilleThetaN.html) for more details.
