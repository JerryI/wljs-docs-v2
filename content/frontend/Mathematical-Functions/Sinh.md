---
title: Sinh
---

`Sinh[z]` gives the hyperbolic sine of `z`, defined as $(e^z - e^{-z})/2$.

## Examples

Compute hyperbolic sine values:

```wolfram
Sinh[0]
(* 0 *)

Sinh[1.0]
(* 1.1752 *)

Sinh[Log[2]]
(* 3/4 *)
```
