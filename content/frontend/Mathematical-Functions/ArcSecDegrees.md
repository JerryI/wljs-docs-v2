---
title: ArcSecDegrees
---

`ArcSecDegrees[z]` gives the arc secant in degrees of the complex number z.

## Examples

Get arc secant in degrees:

```wolfram
ArcSecDegrees[2]
(* 60 *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
ArcSecDegrees[1.5]
(* 48.1897 *)
```
