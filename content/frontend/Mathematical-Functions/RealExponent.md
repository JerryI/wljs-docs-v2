---
title: RealExponent
---

`RealExponent[x]` gives log₁₀(|x|).

- `RealExponent[x, b]` gives log_b(|x|).

## Examples

```wolfram
RealExponent[1000]
```

```wolfram
RealExponent[8, 2]
```

```wolfram
RealExponent[100, 10]
```

*See the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/RealExponent.html) for more details.*
