---
title: MathieuSPrime
---

`MathieuSPrime[a, q, z]` gives the derivative with respect to *z* of the odd Mathieu function with characteristic value *a* and parameter *q*.

## Examples

```wolfram
MathieuSPrime[1, 0.5, 0.3]
```
