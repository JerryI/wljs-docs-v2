---
title: CoulombH1
---

`CoulombH1[l, η, r]` gives the outgoing irregular Coulomb wavefunction Hl(+)(η, r).

## Examples

Compute outgoing Coulomb wavefunction:

```wolfram
CoulombH1[0, 1, 2.0]
```
