---
title: EllipticF
---

`EllipticF[ϕ, m]` gives the elliptic integral of the first kind F(ϕ|m).

## Examples

Evaluate an elliptic integral:

```wolfram
EllipticF[Pi/4, 1/2]
(* 0.804366 *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
N[EllipticF[Pi/6, 0.3]]
(* 0.525604 *)
```
