---
title: WeierstrassPPrime
---

`WeierstrassPPrime[u,{g2,g3}]` gives the derivative of the Weierstrass elliptic function ℘(u;g2,g3).

## Examples

Compute the derivative of the Weierstrass P function:

```wolfram
WeierstrassPPrime[0.5, {1, 2}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/WeierstrassPPrime.html) for more details.*
