---
title: Minus
---

`-x` is the arithmetic negation of x.

## Examples

Negate a number:

```wolfram
-5
(* -5 *)
```

Negate an expression:

```wolfram
-(a + b)
(* -a - b *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Minus.html) for more details.
