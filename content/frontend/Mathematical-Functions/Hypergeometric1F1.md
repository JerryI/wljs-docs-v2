---
title: Hypergeometric1F1
---

`Hypergeometric1F1[a, b, z]` is the Kummer confluent hypergeometric function $_1F_1(a;b;z)$.

## Examples

Compute the function:

```wolfram
Hypergeometric1F1[1, 2, 1.0]
(* 1.71828 *)
```

Special values:

```wolfram
Hypergeometric1F1[1, 1, z]
(* E^z *)
```
