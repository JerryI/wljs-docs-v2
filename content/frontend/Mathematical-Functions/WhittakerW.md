---
title: WhittakerW
---

`WhittakerW[k, m, z]` gives the Whittaker function $W_{k,m}(z)$.

## Examples

Evaluate the Whittaker W function:

```wolfram
WhittakerW[1, 0.5, 2.0]
(* 0.27067 *)
```

Plot the function:

```wolfram
Plot[WhittakerW[0.5, 0.25, x], {x, 0, 5}]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/WhittakerW.html) for more details.
