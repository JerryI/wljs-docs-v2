---
title: EllipticExpPrime
---

`EllipticExpPrime[u, {a, b}]` gives the derivative of `EllipticExp[u, {a, b}]` with respect to u.

## Examples

Compute elliptic exponential derivative:

```wolfram
EllipticExpPrime[1.5, {-1, 0}]
```
