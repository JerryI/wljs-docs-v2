---
title: HankelTransform
---

`HankelTransform[expr, r, s]` gives the Hankel transform of order 0 for expr.

`HankelTransform[expr, r, s, ν]` gives the Hankel transform of order ν for expr.

## Examples

```wolfram
HankelTransform[r^2 Exp[-r], r, s]
(* (2 (4 - s^2))/(1 + s^2)^(5/2) *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/HankelTransform.html) for more details.
