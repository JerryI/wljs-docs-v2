---
title: BesselYZero
---

`BesselYZero[n, k]` represents the kth zero of the Bessel function of the second kind Yn(x).

`BesselYZero[n, k, x0]` represents the kth zero greater than x0.

## Examples

Find zeros of Bessel Y:

```wolfram
N[BesselYZero[0, 1]]
(* 0.893577 *)
```

```wolfram
N[BesselYZero[1, 2]]
(* 5.4297 *)
```
