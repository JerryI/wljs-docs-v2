---
title: WeierstrassE1
---

`WeierstrassE1[{g2,g3}]` gives the value e1 of the Weierstrass elliptic function ℘ at the half-period ω1(g2,g3).

## Examples

Compute e1 for given invariants:

```wolfram
WeierstrassE1[{4, 0}]
(* 1 *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
N[WeierstrassE1[{1, 2}]]
(* -0.6299... *)
```
