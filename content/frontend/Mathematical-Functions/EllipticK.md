---
title: EllipticK
---

`EllipticK[m]` gives the complete elliptic integral of the first kind K(m).

## Examples

Evaluate complete elliptic integral:

```wolfram
EllipticK[0.5]
(* 1.85407 *)
```

Symbolic evaluation:

```wolfram
EllipticK[0]
(* Pi/2 *)
```
