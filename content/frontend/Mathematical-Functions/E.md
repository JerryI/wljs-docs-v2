title: E
---

`E` is the exponential constant (base of natural logarithms), with numerical value ≃2.71828.

## Examples

Get the numerical value:

```wolfram
N[E]
(* 2.71828 *)
```

Use in exponential function:

```wolfram
E^(I Pi) + 1
(* 0 *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/E.html) for more details.
