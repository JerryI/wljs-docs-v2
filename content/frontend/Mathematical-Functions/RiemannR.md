---
title: RiemannR
---

`RiemannR[x]` gives the Riemann prime counting function R(x).

## Examples

```wolfram
RiemannR[100]
```

```wolfram
Plot[{RiemannR[x], PrimePi[x]}, {x, 2, 100}]
```
