---
title: Sec
---

`Sec[z]` gives the secant of z.

## Examples

Compute secant:

```wolfram
Sec[0]
(* 1 *)
```

Secant at an angle:

```wolfram
Sec[Pi/4]
(* Sqrt[2] *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Sec.html) for more details.
