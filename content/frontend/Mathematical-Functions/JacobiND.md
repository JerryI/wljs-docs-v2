---
title: JacobiND
---

`JacobiND[u, m]` gives the Jacobi elliptic function nd(u|m).

## Examples

```wolfram
JacobiND[0.5, 0.3]
```

```wolfram
Plot[JacobiND[u, 0.5], {u, 0, 4}]
```
