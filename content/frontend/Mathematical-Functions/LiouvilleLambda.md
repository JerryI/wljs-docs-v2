---
title: LiouvilleLambda
---

`LiouvilleLambda[n]` gives the Liouville function λ(n).

## Examples

Calculate the Liouville function:

```wolfram
LiouvilleLambda[12]
(* 1 *)
```

For prime numbers, λ(p) = -1:

```wolfram
LiouvilleLambda[7]
(* -1 *)
```

Table of Liouville values:

```wolfram
Table[LiouvilleLambda[n], {n, 1, 20}]
```
