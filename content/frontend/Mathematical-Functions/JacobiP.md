---
title: JacobiP
---

`JacobiP[n, a, b, x]` gives the Jacobi polynomial $P_n^{(a,b)}(x)$.

## Examples

Jacobi polynomial:

```wolfram
JacobiP[3, 1, 2, x]
```

Evaluate at a point:

```wolfram
JacobiP[4, 0, 0, 0.5] // N
(* -0.289063 *)
```
