---
title: NevilleThetaC
---

`NevilleThetaC[z, m]` gives the Neville theta function ϑc(z|m).

## Examples

```wolfram
NevilleThetaC[0.5, 0.3]
```

```wolfram
Plot3D[NevilleThetaC[x + I y, 0.5], {x, -2, 2}, {y, -2, 2}]
```
