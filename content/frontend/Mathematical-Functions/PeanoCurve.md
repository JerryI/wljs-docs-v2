---
title: PeanoCurve
---

`PeanoCurve[n]` gives the line segments representing the nth-step Peano curve.

## Examples

Draw a Peano curve:
```wolfram
Graphics[PeanoCurve[3]]
```

Higher order curve:
```wolfram
Graphics[{Thick, PeanoCurve[4]}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/PeanoCurve.html) for more details.*
