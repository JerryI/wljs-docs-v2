title: AppellF2
---

`AppellF2[a,b1,b2,c1,c2,x,y]` is the Appell hypergeometric function of two variables F2(a;b1,b2;c1,c2;x,y).

## Examples

```wolfram
AppellF2[1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 0.1, 0.2]
```
