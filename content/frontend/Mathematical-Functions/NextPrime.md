---
title: NextPrime
---

`NextPrime[x]` gives the smallest prime above *x*.

- `NextPrime[x, k]` gives the *k*th-next prime above *x*.

## Examples

```wolfram
NextPrime[100]
(* 101 *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/NextPrime.html) for more details.*
