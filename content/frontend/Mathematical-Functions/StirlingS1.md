---
title: StirlingS1
---

`StirlingS1[n, m]` gives the Stirling number of the first kind, related to permutations with cycles.

## Examples

Stirling numbers of the first kind:

```wolfram
Table[StirlingS1[4, k], {k, 0, 4}]
(* {0, 6, -11, 6, -1} *)
```

Single value:

```wolfram
StirlingS1[5, 2]
(* 50 *)
```
