---
title: ShannonWavelet
---

`ShannonWavelet[]` represents the Shannon wavelet evaluated on the equally spaced interval {-10, 10}.

- `ShannonWavelet[lim]` represents the Shannon wavelet evaluated on the equally spaced interval {-lim, lim}.

## Examples

```wolfram
ShannonWavelet[]
```

```wolfram
Plot[ShannonWavelet[][x], {x, -5, 5}]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/ShannonWavelet.html) for more details.
