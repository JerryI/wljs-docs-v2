---
title: Factorial2
---

`n!!` or `Factorial2[n]` gives the double factorial of n (product of every other number from 1 or 2 to n).

## Examples

Double factorial of odd numbers:

```wolfram
7!!
(* 105 - equals 7×5×3×1 *)
```

Double factorial of even numbers:

```wolfram
8!!
(* 384 - equals 8×6×4×2 *)
```

Compare with regular factorial:

```wolfram
{5!, 5!!}
(* {120, 15} *)
```
