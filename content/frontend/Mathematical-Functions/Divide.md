---
title: Divide
---

`x/y` or `Divide[x, y]` is equivalent to x y^-1.

## Examples

Basic division:

```wolfram
Divide[10, 2]
(* 5 *)
```

Symbolic division:

```wolfram
Divide[a + b, c]
(* (a + b)/c *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Divide.html) for more details.*
