---
title: Sign
---

`Sign[x]` gives $-1$, $0$, or $1$ depending on whether `x` is negative, zero, or positive.

## Examples

Get the sign of numbers:

```wolfram
Sign[-5]
(* -1 *)

Sign[0]
(* 0 *)

Sign[3.14]
(* 1 *)

(* Apply to a list *)
Sign[{-2, 0, 5}]
(* {-1, 0, 1} *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Sign.html) for more details.
