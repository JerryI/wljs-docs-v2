---
title: HarmonicNumber
---

`HarmonicNumber[n]` gives the nth harmonic number Hn.

`HarmonicNumber[n,r]` gives the harmonic number Hn(r) of order r.

## Examples

Compute the 10th harmonic number:

```wolfram
HarmonicNumber[10]
(* 7381/2520 *)
```
