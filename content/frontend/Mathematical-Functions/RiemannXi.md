---
title: RiemannXi
---

`RiemannXi[s]` gives the Riemann xi function ξ(s).

## Examples

Compute Riemann xi:
```wolfram
RiemannXi[1/2 + 14.1 I]
```

Plot the function:
```wolfram
Plot[RiemannXi[1/2 + I t], {t, 0, 50}]
```
