---
title: MinimumTimeIncrement
---

`MinimumTimeIncrement[tseries]` gives the minimum time increment in the time series tseries.

## Examples

```wolfram
ts = TimeSeries[{1, 2, 4, 7}, {0, 1, 3, 4}];
MinimumTimeIncrement[ts]
```
