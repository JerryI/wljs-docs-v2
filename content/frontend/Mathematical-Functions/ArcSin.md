---
title: ArcSin
---

`ArcSin[z]` gives the arc sine (inverse sine) of `z`, returning a value in the range $-\frac{\pi}{2}$ to $\frac{\pi}{2}$.

## Examples

Compute the arc sine of common values:

```wolfram
ArcSin[1/2]
(* Pi/6 *)

ArcSin[1]
(* Pi/2 *)

N[ArcSin[0.5]]
(* 0.523599 *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/ArcSin.html) for more details.
