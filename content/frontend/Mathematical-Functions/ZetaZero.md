---
title: ZetaZero
---

`ZetaZero[k]` represents the kth zero of the Riemann zeta function on the critical line.

`ZetaZero[k, t]` represents the kth zero with imaginary part greater than t.

## Examples

```wolfram
N[ZetaZero[1]]
(* 0.5 + 14.1347 I *)
```
