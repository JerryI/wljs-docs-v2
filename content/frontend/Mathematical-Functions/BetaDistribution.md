---
title: BetaDistribution
---

`BetaDistribution[α, β]` represents a continuous beta distribution with shape parameters α and β.

## Examples

Define a beta distribution:

```wolfram
dist = BetaDistribution[2, 5]
```

Compute the mean:

```wolfram
Mean[BetaDistribution[2, 5]]
(* 2/7 *)
```
