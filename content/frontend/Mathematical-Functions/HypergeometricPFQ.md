---
title: HypergeometricPFQ
---

`HypergeometricPFQ[{a1,…,ap},{b1,…,bq},z]` is the generalized hypergeometric function pFq(a;b;z).

## Examples

Evaluate a generalized hypergeometric function:

```wolfram
HypergeometricPFQ[{1, 2}, {3}, 0.5]
(* 1.54518 *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/HypergeometricPFQ.html) for more details.*
