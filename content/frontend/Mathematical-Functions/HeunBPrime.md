---
title: HeunBPrime
---

`HeunBPrime[q, α, γ, δ, ϵ, z]` gives the z-derivative of the HeunB function.

## Examples

```wolfram
HeunBPrime[1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 0.5]
```

```wolfram
Plot[HeunBPrime[1, 1, 1, 1, 1, x], {x, 0, 1}]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/HeunBPrime.html) for more details.
