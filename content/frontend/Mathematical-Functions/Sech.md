---
title: Sech
---

`Sech[z]` gives the hyperbolic secant of z.

## Examples

Compute hyperbolic secant:

```wolfram
Sech[0]
(* 1 *)
```

Symbolic evaluation:

```wolfram
Sech[Log[2]]
(* 4/5 *)
```
