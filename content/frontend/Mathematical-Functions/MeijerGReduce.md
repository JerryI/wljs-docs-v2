---
title: MeijerGReduce
---

`MeijerGReduce[expr, x]` attempts to reduce expr to a single MeijerG object as a function of x.

## Examples

```wolfram
MeijerGReduce[Exp[-x], x]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/MeijerGReduce.html) for more details.
