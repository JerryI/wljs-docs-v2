---
title: ChebyshevU
---

`ChebyshevU[n, x]` gives the Chebyshev polynomial of the second kind U_n(x).

## Examples

Chebyshev U polynomials:

```wolfram
ChebyshevU[0, x]
(* 1 *)

ChebyshevU[2, x]
(* -1 + 4*x^2 *)

ChebyshevU[3, x]
(* -4*x + 8*x^3 *)
```

Numerical value:

```wolfram
ChebyshevU[4, 0.5]
(* -1. *)
```
