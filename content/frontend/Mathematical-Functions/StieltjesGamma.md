---
title: StieltjesGamma
---

`StieltjesGamma[n]` gives the Stieltjes constant γn.

`StieltjesGamma[n, a]` gives the generalized Stieltjes constant γn(a).

## Examples

The Euler-Mascheroni constant:

```wolfram
StieltjesGamma[0]
(* EulerGamma *)
```

Higher Stieltjes constants:

```wolfram
N[StieltjesGamma[1]]
(* -0.0728158 *)
```
