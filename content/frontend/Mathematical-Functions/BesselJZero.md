---
title: BesselJZero
---

`BesselJZero[n, k]` represents the kth zero of the Bessel function Jn(x).

`BesselJZero[n, k, x0]` represents the kth zero greater than x0.

## Examples

Find zeros of Bessel J:

```wolfram
N[BesselJZero[0, 1]]
(* 2.40483 *)
```

```wolfram
N[BesselJZero[1, 2]]
(* 7.01559 *)
```
