---
title: Hypergeometric2F1
---

`Hypergeometric2F1[a, b, c, z]` is the hypergeometric function 2F1(a,b;c;z).

## Examples

```wolfram
Hypergeometric2F1[1, 2, 3, 0.5]
(* 1.54518 *)
```

```wolfram
Hypergeometric2F1[1/2, 1/2, 1, z]
(* represents the complete elliptic integral *)
```
