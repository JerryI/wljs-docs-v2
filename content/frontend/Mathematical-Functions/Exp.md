title: Exp
---

`Exp[z]` gives the exponential of z (e raised to the power z).

## Examples

Exponential of 1:

```wolfram
Exp[1]
(* E *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
Exp[1.0]
(* 2.71828 *)
```

Exponential identities:

```wolfram
Exp[I Pi]
(* -1 *)
```
