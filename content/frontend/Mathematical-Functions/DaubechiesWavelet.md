---
title: DaubechiesWavelet
---

`DaubechiesWavelet[]` represents a Daubechies wavelet of order 2.

`DaubechiesWavelet[n]` represents a Daubechies wavelet of order n.

## Examples

Create a Daubechies wavelet of order 4:

```wolfram
DaubechiesWavelet[4]
```
