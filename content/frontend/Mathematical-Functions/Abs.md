title: Abs
---

`Abs[z]` gives the absolute value of the real or complex number z.

## Examples

Absolute value of real numbers:

```wolfram
Abs[-5]
(* 5 *)
```

Absolute value (modulus) of a complex number:

```wolfram
Abs[3 + 4 I]
(* 5 *)
```
