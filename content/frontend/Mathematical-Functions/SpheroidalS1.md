---
title: SpheroidalS1
---

`SpheroidalS1[n, m, γ, z]` gives the radial spheroidal function S_{n,m}^{(1)}(γ, z) of the first kind.

## Examples

```wolfram
SpheroidalS1[2, 1, 0.5, 0.3]
```

```wolfram
Plot[SpheroidalS1[2, 0, 1, x], {x, 1, 3}]
```
