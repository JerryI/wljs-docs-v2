---
title: SpheroidalQSPrime
---

`SpheroidalQSPrime[n,m,γ,z]` gives the derivative with respect to z of the angular spheroidal function QSn,m(γ,z) of the second kind.

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/SpheroidalQSPrime.html) for more details.
