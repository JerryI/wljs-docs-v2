---
title: DivideBy
---

`x /= c` divides x by c and returns the new value of x.

## Examples

Divide and assign:

```wolfram
x = 100;
x /= 5
(* 20 *)
```

Successive division:

```wolfram
y = 1000;
y /= 10;
y /= 2
(* 50 *)
```
