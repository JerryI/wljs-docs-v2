---
title: AiryAiZero
---

`AiryAiZero[k]` represents the kth zero of the Airy function Ai(x).

`AiryAiZero[k, x0]` represents the kth zero less than x0.

## Examples

Get the first zero of AiryAi:

```wolfram
AiryAiZero[1]
(* -2.33811... *)
```

Find numerical value:

```wolfram
N[AiryAiZero[1]]
(* -2.33811 *)
```
