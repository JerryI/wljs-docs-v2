---
title: ThreeJSymbol
---

`ThreeJSymbol[{j1, m1}, {j2, m2}, {j3, m3}]` gives the values of the Wigner 3-j symbol.

## Examples

Compute a 3-j symbol:

```wolfram
ThreeJSymbol[{1, 0}, {1, 0}, {1, 0}]
(* 0 *)
```

```wolfram
ThreeJSymbol[{1, 1}, {1, -1}, {1, 0}]
```
