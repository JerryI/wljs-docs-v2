---
title: SpheroidalPSPrime
---

`SpheroidalPSPrime[n, m, γ, z]` gives the derivative with respect to z of the angular spheroidal function PS_n,m(γ, z) of the first kind.

## Examples

```wolfram
SpheroidalPSPrime[2, 1, 0.5, 0.3]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/SpheroidalPSPrime.html) for more details.
