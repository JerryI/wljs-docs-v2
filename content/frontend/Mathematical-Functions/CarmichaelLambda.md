---
title: CarmichaelLambda
---

`CarmichaelLambda[n]` gives the Carmichael function λ(n).

## Examples

Compute the Carmichael function:

```wolfram
CarmichaelLambda[12]
(* 2 *)
```

Compare with Euler's totient:

```wolfram
{CarmichaelLambda[100], EulerPhi[100]}
(* {20, 40} *)
```
