---
title: WeierstrassEta3
---

`WeierstrassEta3[{g2,g3}]` gives the value η3 of the Weierstrass zeta function ζ at the half-period ω3(g2,g3).

## Examples

Compute eta3 for given invariants:

```wolfram
WeierstrassEta3[{4, 0}]
(* Complex result *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
N[WeierstrassEta3[{1, 2}]]
(* Numerical result *)
```
