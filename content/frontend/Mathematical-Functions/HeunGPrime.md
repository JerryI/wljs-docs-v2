title: HeunGPrime
---

`HeunGPrime[a, q, α, β, γ, δ, z]` gives the z-derivative of the `HeunG` function.

## Examples

```wolfram
HeunGPrime[2, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 0.5]
```
