---
title: Sin
---

`Sin[z]` gives the sine of z.

## Examples

Sine of common angles:

```wolfram
Sin[Pi/6]
(* 1/2 *)

Sin[Pi/2]
(* 1 *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
Sin[1.0]
(* 0.841471 *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Sin.html) for more details.
