---
title: FresnelG
---

`FresnelG[z]` gives the Fresnel auxiliary function G(z).

## Examples

Evaluate the Fresnel G function:

```wolfram
FresnelG[2]
```

Plot the function:

```wolfram
Plot[FresnelG[x], {x, 0, 5}]
```

Compute numerically:

```wolfram
N[FresnelG[1]]
```
