---
title: StruveH
---

`StruveH[n, z]` gives the Struve function Hₙ(z).

## Examples

Compute Struve H function:
```wolfram
StruveH[1, 2.5]
```

Plot the function:
```wolfram
Plot[StruveH[0, x], {x, 0, 10}]
```
