---
title: PrimeZetaP
---

`PrimeZetaP[s]` gives prime zeta function P(s).

## Examples

Evaluate prime zeta function:

```wolfram
PrimeZetaP[2]
```

Numerical value:

```wolfram
N[PrimeZetaP[2]]
(* 0.452247 *)
```

Plot the function:

```wolfram
Plot[PrimeZetaP[s], {s, 1.1, 5}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/PrimeZetaP.html) for more details.*
