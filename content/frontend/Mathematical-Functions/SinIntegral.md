---
title: SinIntegral
---

`SinIntegral[z]` gives the sine integral function Si(z).

## Examples

Evaluate at a numeric value:

```wolfram
SinIntegral[2.0]
(* 1.60541 *)
```

Plot the function:

```wolfram
Plot[SinIntegral[x], {x, -10, 10}]
```
