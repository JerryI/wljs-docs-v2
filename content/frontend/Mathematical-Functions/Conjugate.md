---
title: Conjugate
---

`Conjugate[z]` gives the complex conjugate of the complex number z.

## Examples

Complex conjugate of a number:

```wolfram
Conjugate[3 + 4 I]
(* 3 - 4 I *)
```

Conjugate of a purely imaginary number:

```wolfram
Conjugate[5 I]
(* -5 I *)
```

Real numbers are unchanged:

```wolfram
Conjugate[7]
(* 7 *)
```
