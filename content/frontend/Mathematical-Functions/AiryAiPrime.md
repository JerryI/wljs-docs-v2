---
title: AiryAiPrime
---

`AiryAiPrime[z]` gives the derivative of the Airy function Ai'(z).

## Examples

Evaluate the derivative of the Airy function:

```wolfram
AiryAiPrime[0]
(* -(1/(3^(1/3) Gamma[1/3])) *)
```

Plot the derivative of Ai:

```wolfram
Plot[AiryAiPrime[x], {x, -10, 5}]
```
