---
title: ZernikeR
---

`ZernikeR[n, m, r]` gives the radial Zernike polynomial $R_n^m(r)$.

## Examples

Evaluate a Zernike polynomial:

```wolfram
ZernikeR[4, 2, r]
(* 4 r^4 - 3 r^2 *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
ZernikeR[3, 1, 0.5]
(* -0.125 *)
```
