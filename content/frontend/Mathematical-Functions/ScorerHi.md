---
title: ScorerHi
---

`ScorerHi[z]` gives the Scorer function Hi(z).

## Examples

Evaluate at a point:

```wolfram
ScorerHi[1.0]
```

Plot the function:

```wolfram
Plot[ScorerHi[x], {x, -10, 5}]
```

Symbolic value:

```wolfram
ScorerHi[0]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/ScorerHi.html) for more details.*
