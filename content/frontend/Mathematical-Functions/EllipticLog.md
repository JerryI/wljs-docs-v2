---
title: EllipticLog
---

`EllipticLog[{x, y}, {a, b}]` gives the generalized logarithm associated with the elliptic curve y² = x³ + ax² + bx.

## Examples

Compute elliptic logarithm:

```wolfram
EllipticLog[{2, 3}, {-1, 0}]
```
