---
title: Quotient
---

`Quotient[m, n]` gives the integer quotient of m and n.

## Examples

Integer division:

```wolfram
Quotient[17, 5]
(* 3 *)
```

```wolfram
Quotient[100, 7]
(* 14 *)
```
