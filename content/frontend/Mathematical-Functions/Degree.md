---
title: Degree
---

`Degree` gives the number of radians in one degree. It has a numerical value of π/180.

## Examples

Convert degrees to radians:

```wolfram
45 Degree
(* Pi/4 *)
```

Use in trigonometric functions:

```wolfram
Sin[30 Degree]
(* 1/2 *)
```

Numerical value:

```wolfram
N[Degree]
(* 0.0174533 *)
```
