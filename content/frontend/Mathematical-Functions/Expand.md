---
title: Expand
---

`Expand[expr]` expands out products and positive integer powers in expr.

## Examples

Expand a polynomial:

```wolfram
Expand[(x + y)^2]
(* x^2 + 2 x y + y^2 *)
```

Expand products:

```wolfram
Expand[(a + b)(c + d)]
(* a c + a d + b c + b d *)
```
