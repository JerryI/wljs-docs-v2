---
title: HeunT
---

`HeunT[q, α, γ, δ, ϵ, z]` gives the tri-confluent Heun function.

## Examples

```wolfram
HeunT[1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 0.5]
```

```wolfram
Plot[HeunT[1, 1, 1, 1, 1, x], {x, 0, 1}]
```
