---
title: BesselI
---

`BesselI[n, z]` gives the modified Bessel function of the first kind I_n(z).

## Examples

Modified Bessel function:

```wolfram
BesselI[0, 0]
(* 1 *)

BesselI[1, 0]
(* 0 *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
N[BesselI[0, 1]]
(* 1.26607 *)

N[BesselI[2, 3]]
(* 2.24521 *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/BesselI.html) for more details.*
