---
title: AppellF4
---

`AppellF4[a, b, c1, c2, x, y]` is the Appell hypergeometric function of two variables F4(a; b; c1, c2; x, y).

## Examples

Evaluate the Appell F4 function:

```wolfram
AppellF4[1, 2, 3, 4, 0.1, 0.1]
```
