---
title: FresnelS
---

`FresnelS[z]` gives the Fresnel sine integral S(z).

## Examples

Compute Fresnel S:

```wolfram
FresnelS[1.0]
(* 0.438259 *)
```

Plot the function:

```wolfram
Plot[FresnelS[x], {x, 0, 5}]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/FresnelS.html) for more details.
