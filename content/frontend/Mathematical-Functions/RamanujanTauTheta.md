---
title: RamanujanTauTheta
---

`RamanujanTauTheta[t]` gives the Ramanujan tau theta function θ(t).

## Examples

Evaluate the Ramanujan tau theta function:

```wolfram
RamanujanTauTheta[0.5]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/RamanujanTauTheta.html) for more details.
