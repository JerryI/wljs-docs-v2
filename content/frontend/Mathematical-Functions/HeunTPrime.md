---
title: HeunTPrime
---

`HeunTPrime[q, α, γ, δ, ϵ, z]` gives the z-derivative of the HeunT function.

## Examples

```wolfram
HeunTPrime[1, 2, 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 0.5]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/HeunTPrime.html) for more details.*
