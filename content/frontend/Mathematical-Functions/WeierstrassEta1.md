---
title: WeierstrassEta1
---

`WeierstrassEta1[{g2, g3}]` gives the value η1 of the Weierstrass zeta function ζ at the half-period ω1(g2, g3).

## Examples

```wolfram
WeierstrassEta1[{1, 0}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/WeierstrassEta1.html) for more details.*
