---
title: GCD
---

`GCD[n1, n2, ...]` gives the greatest common divisor of the integers.

## Examples

Find GCD of two numbers:

```wolfram
GCD[12, 18]
(* 6 *)
```

GCD of multiple numbers:

```wolfram
GCD[24, 36, 48]
(* 12 *)
```

GCD of coprime numbers:

```wolfram
GCD[7, 11]
(* 1 *)
```
