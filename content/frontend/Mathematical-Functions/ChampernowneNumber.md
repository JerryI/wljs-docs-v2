---
title: ChampernowneNumber
---

`ChampernowneNumber[b]` gives the base-b Champernowne number.

`ChampernowneNumber[]` gives the base-10 Champernowne number.

## Examples

The base-10 Champernowne constant:

```wolfram
N[ChampernowneNumber[], 50]
(* 0.12345678910111213141516171819202122232425... *)
```

Base-2 Champernowne number:

```wolfram
N[ChampernowneNumber[2], 20]
(* 0.86224615... *)
```
