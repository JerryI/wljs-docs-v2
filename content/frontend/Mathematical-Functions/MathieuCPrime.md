---
title: MathieuCPrime
---

`MathieuCPrime[a, q, z]` gives the derivative with respect to z of the even Mathieu function with characteristic value a and parameter q.

## Examples

```wolfram
MathieuCPrime[1, 0.5, 0.3]
```

```wolfram
Plot[MathieuCPrime[1, 1, z], {z, 0, 2 Pi}]
```
