---
title: ArcCotDegrees
---

`ArcCotDegrees[z]` gives the arc cotangent in degrees of the complex number z.

## Examples

Get arc cotangent in degrees:

```wolfram
ArcCotDegrees[1]
(* 45 *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
ArcCotDegrees[2.0]
(* 26.5651 *)
```
