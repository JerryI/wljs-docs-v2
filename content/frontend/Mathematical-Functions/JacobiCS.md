---
title: JacobiCS
---

`JacobiCS[u, m]` gives the Jacobi elliptic function cs(u|m).

## Examples

```wolfram
JacobiCS[0, 0.5]
(* ComplexInfinity *)
```

```wolfram
N[JacobiCS[1.0, 0.5]]
(* 0.740839 *)
```
