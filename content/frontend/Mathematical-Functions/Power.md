---
title: Power
---

`x^y` gives x to the power y.

## Examples

Exponentiation:

```wolfram
2^10
(* 1024 *)
```

Fractional powers:

```wolfram
8^(1/3)
(* 2 *)
```

Symbolic:

```wolfram
x^2 * x^3
(* x^5 *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Power.html) for more details.*
