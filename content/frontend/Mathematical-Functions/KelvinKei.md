---
title: KelvinKei
---

`KelvinKei[z]` gives the Kelvin function kei(z).

`KelvinKei[n, z]` gives the Kelvin function kein(z).

## Examples

Evaluate the Kelvin kei function:

```wolfram
KelvinKei[2.0]
(* 0.11903 *)
```

Plot the function:

```wolfram
Plot[KelvinKei[x], {x, 0, 10}]
```
