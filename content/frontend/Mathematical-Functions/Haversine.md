---
title: Haversine
---

`Haversine[z]` gives the haversine function hav(z) = (1 - cos(z))/2.

## Examples

Compute haversine:

```wolfram
Haversine[Pi]
(* 1 *)
```

Haversine of 0:

```wolfram
Haversine[0]
(* 0 *)
```
