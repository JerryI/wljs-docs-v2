---
title: HurwitzLerchPhi
---

`HurwitzLerchPhi[z,s,a]` gives the Hurwitz–Lerch transcendent Φ(z,s,a).

## Examples

Evaluate the Hurwitz-Lerch transcendent:

```wolfram
HurwitzLerchPhi[1/2, 2, 1]
(* Pi^2/12 - Log[2]^2/2 *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
N[HurwitzLerchPhi[0.5, 2, 3]]
(* 0.138629 *)
```
