---
title: PositivelyOrientedPoints
---

`PositivelyOrientedPoints[{p1, p2, p3, …, pd+1}]` tests whether the sequence of points p1, p2, p3, …, pd+1 is positively oriented.

## Examples

```wolfram
PositivelyOrientedPoints[{{0, 0}, {1, 0}, {0, 1}}]
```
