---
title: NotHumpDownHump
---

`NotHumpDownHump[x, y, ...]` displays as x ≎̸ y ≎̸ ....

## Examples

```wolfram
NotHumpDownHump[a, b, c]
```

```wolfram
a \[NotHumpDownHump] b
```
