---
title: FindFundamentalCycles
---

`FindFundamentalCycles[g]` finds fundamental cycles of the graph g.

## Examples

```wolfram
FindFundamentalCycles[CycleGraph[5]]
```

```wolfram
FindFundamentalCycles[CompleteGraph[4]]
```
