---
title: NonPositiveIntegers
---

`NonPositiveIntegers` represents the domain of non-positive integers, as in x ∈ NonPositiveIntegers.

## Examples

Test membership:

```wolfram
Element[0, NonPositiveIntegers]
(* True *)
```

Positive fails:

```wolfram
Element[1, NonPositiveIntegers]
(* False *)
```
