---
title: Complexes
---

`Complexes` represents the domain of complex numbers, as in x∈Complexes.

## Examples

Specify that a variable is complex:

```wolfram
Element[z, Complexes]
(* z ∈ Complexes *)
```

Solve over the complex numbers:

```wolfram
Solve[x^2 + 1 == 0, x, Complexes]
(* {{x -> -I}, {x -> I}} *)
```
