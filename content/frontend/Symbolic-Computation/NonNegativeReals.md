---
title: NonNegativeReals
---

`NonNegativeReals` represents the domain of non-negative real numbers.

## Examples

Test membership:

```wolfram
Element[0, NonNegativeReals]
(* True *)
```

Negative fails:

```wolfram
Element[-0.5, NonNegativeReals]
(* False *)
```
