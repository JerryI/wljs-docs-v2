---
title: Head
---

`Head[expr]` gives the head of expr.

## Examples

Get the head of various expressions:

```wolfram
Head[{1, 2, 3}]
(* List *)

Head[f[x, y]]
(* f *)

Head[5]
(* Integer *)

Head[3.14]
(* Real *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Head.html) for more details.*
