---
title: UnequalTo
---

`UnequalTo[y]` is an operator form that yields x ≠ y when applied to an expression x.

## Examples

```wolfram
UnequalTo[5][3]
```

```wolfram
Select[{1, 2, 3, 4, 5}, UnequalTo[3]]
```
