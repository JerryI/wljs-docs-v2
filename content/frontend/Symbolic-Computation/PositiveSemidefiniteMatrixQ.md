---
title: PositiveSemidefiniteMatrixQ
---

`PositiveSemidefiniteMatrixQ[m]` gives `True` if m is explicitly positive semidefinite, and `False` otherwise.

## Examples

```wolfram
PositiveSemidefiniteMatrixQ[{{1, 0}, {0, 1}}]
```
