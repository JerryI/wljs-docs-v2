---
title: PositiveIntegers
---

`PositiveIntegers` represents the domain of strictly positive integers, as in x ∈ PositiveIntegers.

## Examples

Test membership:

```wolfram
Element[5, PositiveIntegers]
(* True *)
```

Zero is not positive:

```wolfram
Element[0, PositiveIntegers]
(* False *)
```
