---
title: GreaterSlantEqual
---

`GreaterSlantEqual[x, y, …]` displays as x⩾y⩾….

## Examples

```wolfram
GreaterSlantEqual[a, b, c]
```

```wolfram
GreaterSlantEqual[10, 5, 5]
```
