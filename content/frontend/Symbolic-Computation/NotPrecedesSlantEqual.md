---
title: NotPrecedesSlantEqual
---

`NotPrecedesSlantEqual[x, y, ...]` displays as x⋠y⋠….

## Examples

```wolfram
NotPrecedesSlantEqual[a, b, c]
(* a ⋠ b ⋠ c *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/NotPrecedesSlantEqual.html) for more details.*
