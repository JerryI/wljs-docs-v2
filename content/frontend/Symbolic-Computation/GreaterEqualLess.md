---
title: GreaterEqualLess
---

`GreaterEqualLess[x, y, …]` displays as x⋛y⋛….

## Examples

```wolfram
GreaterEqualLess[a, b, c]
```

```wolfram
GreaterEqualLess[1, 2, 3]
```
