---
title: NegativeIntegers
---

`NegativeIntegers` represents the domain of strictly negative integers, as in x ∈ NegativeIntegers.

## Examples

Test membership:

```wolfram
Element[-5, NegativeIntegers]
(* True *)
```

Zero is not a negative integer:

```wolfram
Element[0, NegativeIntegers]
(* False *)
```
