```wolfram
MeshToonMaterial[]
```

uses lighting model for [MeshMaterial](../Graphics3D/MeshMaterial) to emulate cell-shading effect

```wolfram
Graphics3D[{MeshMaterial[MeshToonMaterial[]], Roughness[0], Sphere[], PointLight[Red, {1.5075, 4.1557, 2.6129}, "Intensity"->50],  PointLight[Cyan, {-2.268, -2.144, 3.1635}, "Intensity"->50]}, "Lighting"->None]
```

<Callout type="tip">
An effect comes better when used together with [SpotLight](../Graphics3D/SpotLight) and minimal ambient lighting
</Callout>
