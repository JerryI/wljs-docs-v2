```wolfram
MeshPhysicalMaterial[]
```

a default material used in [MeshMaterial](../Graphics3D/MeshMaterial). The most accurate material, that can be parameterized with

### [Roughness](../Graphics3D/Roughness)
### [Emissive](../Graphics3D/Emissive)

### [Metalness](../Graphics3D/Metalness)

## Example

```wolfram
Graphics3D[{MeshMaterial[MeshPhysicalMaterial[]], Roughness[0], Sphere[], PointLight[Red, {1.5075, 4.1557, 2.6129}, "Intensity"->50],  PointLight[Cyan, {-2.268, -2.144, 3.1635}, "Intensity"->50]}, "Lighting"->None]
```
