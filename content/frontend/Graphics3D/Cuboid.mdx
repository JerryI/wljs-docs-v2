```wolfram
Cuboid[pmin_, pmax_]
```
draws a hypercube from `pmin` to `pmax`

```wolfram
Graphics3D[
 Table[{EdgeForm[Opacity[.3]], Hue[RandomReal[], 1, 0.5], 
   Cuboid[RandomReal[4, 3]]}, {40}]]
```
