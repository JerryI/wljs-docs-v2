```wolfram
MeshMaterial[material_]
```

specifies the material used for 3D lighting in [Graphics3D](../Graphics3D/Graphics3D)

__This is scope-type parameter__ like [Opacity](../Graphics3D/Opacity), [RGBColor](../Graphics3D/RGBColor) and can be applied separately for each object.
