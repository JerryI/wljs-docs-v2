```wolfram
MeshMaterial[material_]
```

specifies the material used for 3D lighting in [Graphics3D](../Graphics3D/Graphics3D)

<Callout type="note">
`MeshMaterial` remains supported. For best compatibility with Wolfram
Mathematica and access to future material features, use
[MaterialShading](./MaterialShading). Replace `MeshMaterial[material]` with
`MaterialShading[material]`.
</Callout>

__This is scope-type parameter__ like [Opacity](../Graphics3D/Opacity), [RGBColor](../Graphics3D/RGBColor) and can be applied separately for each object.
