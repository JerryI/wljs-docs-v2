---
title: Hold
---

`Hold[expr]` maintains expr in an unevaluated form.

## Examples

Prevent evaluation:

```wolfram
Hold[1 + 1]
(* Hold[1 + 1] *)
```

Inspect unevaluated:

```wolfram
Hold[2^10]
(* Hold[2^10] *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Hold.html) for more details.
