---
title: Information
---

`Information[expr]` gives information about the expression expr.

`Information[expr, prop]` gives the value of the property prop.

## Examples

Get symbol information:

```wolfram
Information[Sin]
```

Get specific property:

```wolfram
Information[Sin, "Usage"]
```
