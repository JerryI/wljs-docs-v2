---
title: TypeSpecifier
---

`TypeSpecifier[cons]` represents a type.

- `TypeSpecifier[cons][type1, …]` or `cons::[type1, …]` represents a compound type.

## Examples

```wolfram
TypeSpecifier["Integer32"]
```
