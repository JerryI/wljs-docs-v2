---
title: InertEvaluate
---

`InertEvaluate[inertExpr]` evaluates an "InertExpression", returning a new "InertExpression" in compiled code.

## Examples

```wolfram
InertEvaluate[Inactive[Plus][1, 2]]
```

```wolfram
InertEvaluate[Inactive[Sin][Pi]]
```
