---
title: IncrementalFunction
---

```wolfram
IncrementalFunction[fun]
```

represents a compilable function that can pause its execution, preserve its state and resume later.

Incremental functions are processed by compiler functions such as [FunctionCompile](../Programming-Constructs/FunctionCompile). They can yield a sequence of partial results without completing.

## Example

```wolfram
compiled = FunctionCompile[
  IncrementalFunction[
    Function[
      Typed[n, "Integer64"],
      While[True, IncrementalYield[n]]
    ]
  ]
];

instance = compiled[10];
instance["Next"]
```

See the official [Wolfram Language reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/IncrementalFunction.html) for yielding, receiving and managing active instances.
