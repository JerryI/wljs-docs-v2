---
title: Locked
---

`Locked` is an attribute that, once assigned, prevents modification of any attributes of a symbol.

## Examples

Check attributes of a locked symbol:

```wolfram
Attributes[Sin]
(* {Listable, NumericFunction, Protected, Locked} *)
```
