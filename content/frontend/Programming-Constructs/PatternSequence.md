---
title: PatternSequence
---

`PatternSequence[p1, p2, …]` is a pattern object that represents a sequence of arguments matching p1, p2, ….

## Examples

```wolfram
Cases[{{1, 2, 3}, {a, b}}, {PatternSequence[x_, y_], ___}]
```
