---
title: ToExpression
---

`ToExpression[input]` gives the expression obtained by interpreting strings as Wolfram Language input.

## Examples

Convert string to expression:

```wolfram
ToExpression["1 + 2 + 3"]
(* 6 *)
```

Parse symbolic expression:

```wolfram
ToExpression["x^2 + y"]
(* x^2 + y *)
```
