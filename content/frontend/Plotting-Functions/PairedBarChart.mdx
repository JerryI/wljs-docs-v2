---
title: PairedBarChart
---

`PairedBarChart[{y1, y2, …}, {z1, z2, …}]` makes a paired bar chart with bar lengths y1, y2, … and z1, z2, ….

## Examples

Basic paired bar chart:

```wolfram
PairedBarChart[{1, 2, 3}, {2, 3, 1}]
```

With labels:

```wolfram
PairedBarChart[{1, 2, 3}, {2, 3, 1}, ChartLabels -> {"A", "B", "C"}]
```
