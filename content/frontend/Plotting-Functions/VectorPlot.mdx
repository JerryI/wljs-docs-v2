---
title: VectorPlot
---

`VectorPlot[{vx, vy}, {x, xmin, xmax}, {y, ymin, ymax}]` plots a 2D vector field as arrows.

## Examples

Plot vector fields:

```wolfram
(* Simple vector field *)
VectorPlot[{y, -x}, {x, -2, 2}, {y, -2, 2}]

(* Gradient field *)
VectorPlot[{2 x, 2 y}, {x, -2, 2}, {y, -2, 2},
  VectorColorFunction -> "Rainbow"
]
```
