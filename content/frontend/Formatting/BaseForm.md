---
title: BaseForm
---

`BaseForm[expr, n]` prints with the numbers in expr given in base n.

## Examples

Display a number in binary:

```wolfram
BaseForm[255, 2]
(* 11111111₂ *)
```

Display in hexadecimal:

```wolfram
BaseForm[255, 16]
(* ff₁₆ *)
```
