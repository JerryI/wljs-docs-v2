A standard form for representing math, colors, dates, graphics, which comes with visual decorations

__This is a default representation__ used in output cells. You can apply it to expressions before using in [EditorView](../GUI/EditorView) and [InputEditor](../GUI/InputEditor) and [CellPrint](../Cells%20and%20Notebook/CellPrint) and [Interpretation](./Interpretation)

<Callout type="note">`StandardForm` is a decorated `InputForm` in WLJS Notebook</Callout>
