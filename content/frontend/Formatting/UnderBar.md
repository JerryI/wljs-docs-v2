---
title: UnderBar
---

`UnderBar[expr]` displays with a bar under expr.

## Examples

Display an expression with an underbar:

```wolfram
UnderBar[x]
```

In a mathematical expression:

```wolfram
UnderBar[a] + UnderBar[b]
```

Combine with other formatting:

```wolfram
Style[UnderBar[expr], Large, Bold]
```
