---
title: Indexed
---
```wolfram
Indexed[expr_, i_]
```

represents the component of `expr` with index `i` and formats as expected in traditional form.

```wolfram
Indexed[expr_, {i_, j_, ...}]
```

For example

```wolfram
Indexed[x, {1,2}]
```
