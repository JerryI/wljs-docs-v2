---
title: BenktanderWeibullDistribution
---

`BenktanderWeibullDistribution[a, b]` represents a Benktander distribution of type II with parameters a and b.

## Examples

Create distribution:

```wolfram
dist = BenktanderWeibullDistribution[1, 0.5];
PDF[dist, x]
```
