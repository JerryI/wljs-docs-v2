---
title: LevyDistribution
---

`LevyDistribution[μ, σ]` represents a Lévy distribution with location parameter `μ` and dispersion parameter `σ`.

## Examples

```wolfram
LevyDistribution[0, 1]
```

```wolfram
PDF[LevyDistribution[0, 1], x]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/LevyDistribution.html) for more details.*
