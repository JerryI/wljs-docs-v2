---
title: SnDispersion
---

`SnDispersion[list]` gives the Sn statistic of the elements in list.

- `SnDispersion[list, c]` gives the Sn statistic with scaling factor c.

## Examples

```wolfram
SnDispersion[{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 100}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/SnDispersion.html) for more details.*
