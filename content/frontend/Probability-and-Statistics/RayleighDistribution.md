---
title: RayleighDistribution
---

`RayleighDistribution[σ]` represents the Rayleigh distribution with scale parameter σ.

## Examples

```wolfram
Mean[RayleighDistribution[1]]
```

```wolfram
RandomVariate[RayleighDistribution[2], 10]
```
