---
title: BernoulliProcess
---

`BernoulliProcess[p]` represents a Bernoulli process with event probability p.

## Examples

Simulate a Bernoulli process:

```wolfram
RandomFunction[BernoulliProcess[0.3], {0, 20}]
```
