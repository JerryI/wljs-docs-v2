---
title: ErlangB
---

`ErlangB[c, a]` computes the Erlang B loss probability for an M/M/c/c queue.

## Examples

Compute Erlang B probability:

```wolfram
ErlangB[10, 8]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/ErlangB.html) for more details.
