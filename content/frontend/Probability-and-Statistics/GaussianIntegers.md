---
title: GaussianIntegers
---

`GaussianIntegers` is an option for FactorInteger, PrimeQ, Factor, and related functions that specifies whether factorization should be done over Gaussian integers.

## Examples

Factor over Gaussian integers:

```wolfram
FactorInteger[5, GaussianIntegers -> True]
```

Check primality over Gaussian integers:

```wolfram
PrimeQ[5, GaussianIntegers -> True]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/GaussianIntegers.html) for more details.*
