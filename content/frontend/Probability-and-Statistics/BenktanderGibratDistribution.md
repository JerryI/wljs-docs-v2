---
title: BenktanderGibratDistribution
---

`BenktanderGibratDistribution[a, b]` represents a Benktander distribution of type I with parameters a and b.

## Examples

Create distribution:

```wolfram
dist = BenktanderGibratDistribution[1, 0.5];
PDF[dist, x]
```
