---
title: YuleDissimilarity
---

`YuleDissimilarity[u, v]` gives the Yule dissimilarity between Boolean vectors u and v.

## Examples

```wolfram
YuleDissimilarity[{1, 0, 1, 1}, {1, 1, 0, 1}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/YuleDissimilarity.html) for more details.*
