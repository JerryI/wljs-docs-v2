---
title: VarianceEstimatorFunction
---

`VarianceEstimatorFunction` is an option for `LinearModelFit` and `NonlinearModelFit` which specifies the variance estimator to use.

## Examples

```wolfram
LinearModelFit[data, x, x, VarianceEstimatorFunction -> (1 &)]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/VarianceEstimatorFunction.html) for more details.*
