---
title: EditDistance
---

`EditDistance[u,v]` gives the edit or Levenshtein distance between strings, vectors or biomolecular sequences u and v.

## Examples

Find the edit distance between two strings:

```wolfram
EditDistance["kitten", "sitting"]
(* 3 *)
```
