---
title: WeakStationarity
---

`WeakStationarity[proc]` gives conditions for the process proc to be weakly stationary.

## Examples

```wolfram
WeakStationarity[ARProcess[{a}, 1]]
```

```wolfram
WeakStationarity[ARMAProcess[{0.5}, {0.3}, 1]]
```
