---
title: GeometricMean
---

`GeometricMean[data]` gives the geometric mean of the values in data.

## Examples

Geometric mean of a list:

```wolfram
GeometricMean[{2, 4, 8}]
(* 4 *)
```

Compared to arithmetic mean:

```wolfram
{GeometricMean[{1, 9}], Mean[{1, 9}]}
(* {3, 5} *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/GeometricMean.html) for more details.
