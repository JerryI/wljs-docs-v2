---
title: RandomEntity
---

`RandomEntity[spec]` gives a pseudorandom entity with a type determined by the specification *spec*.

- `RandomEntity[spec, n]` gives a list of *n* pseudorandom entities.

## Examples

```wolfram
RandomEntity["Country"]
```
