---
title: CoxianDistribution
---

`CoxianDistribution[{α1, ..., αm-1}, {λ1, ..., λm}]` represents an m-phase Coxian distribution with phase probabilities αi and rates λi.

## Examples

Create a Coxian distribution:

```wolfram
CoxianDistribution[{0.5}, {1, 2}]
```
