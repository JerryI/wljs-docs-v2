---
title: LindleyDistribution
---

`LindleyDistribution[δ]` represents a Lindley distribution with shape parameter δ.

## Examples

Create a Lindley distribution:

```wolfram
LindleyDistribution[2]
(* LindleyDistribution[2] *)
```

Compute the mean:

```wolfram
Mean[LindleyDistribution[2]]
(* 1/2 *)
```
