---
title: BorelTannerDistribution
---

`BorelTannerDistribution[α, n]` represents a Borel–Tanner distribution with shape parameters α and n.

## Examples

Define a Borel-Tanner distribution:

```wolfram
dist = BorelTannerDistribution[0.5, 3]
```

Calculate the mean:

```wolfram
Mean[BorelTannerDistribution[0.5, 3]]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/BorelTannerDistribution.html) for more details.*
