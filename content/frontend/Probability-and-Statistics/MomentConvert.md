---
title: MomentConvert
---

`MomentConvert[mexpr, form]` converts the moment expression mexpr to the specified form.

## Examples

Convert to central moments:
```wolfram
MomentConvert[Moment[2], CentralMoment]
```

Convert to cumulants:
```wolfram
MomentConvert[Moment[3], Cumulant]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/MomentConvert.html) for more details.
