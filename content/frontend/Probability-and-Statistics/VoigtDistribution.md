---
title: VoigtDistribution
---

`VoigtDistribution[δ, σ]` represents Voigt distribution with parameters δ and σ.

## Examples

```wolfram
dist = VoigtDistribution[1, 2]
```

```wolfram
Plot[PDF[dist, x], {x, -10, 10}]
```
