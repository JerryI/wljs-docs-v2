---
title: MovingMedian
---

`MovingMedian[list, r]` gives the moving median of list, computed using spans of r elements.

## Examples

```wolfram
MovingMedian[{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7}, 3]
(* {2, 3, 4, 5, 6} *)
```

```wolfram
MovingMedian[{1, 5, 2, 8, 3}, 3]
(* {2, 5, 3} *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/MovingMedian.html) for more details.
