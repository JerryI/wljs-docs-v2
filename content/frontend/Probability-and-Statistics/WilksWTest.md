---
title: WilksWTest
---

`WilksWTest[m1, m2]` tests whether the matrices m1 and m2 are independent.

`WilksWTest[..., "property"]` returns the value of "property".

## Examples

Test independence of two matrices:

```wolfram
WilksWTest[{{1, 2}, {3, 4}}, {{5, 6}, {7, 8}}]
```
