---
title: LowerTriangularMatrix
---

`LowerTriangularMatrix[lmat]` converts the lower triangular matrix lmat to a structured array.

## Examples

Create a lower triangular matrix:

```wolfram
LowerTriangularMatrix[{{1}, {2, 3}, {4, 5, 6}}]
```
