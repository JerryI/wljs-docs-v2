---
title: InverseWeierstrassP
---

`InverseWeierstrassP[p, {g2, g3}]` gives a value of u for which the Weierstrass function ℘(u; g2, g3) is equal to p.

## Examples

```wolfram
InverseWeierstrassP[1, {0, 1}]
```
