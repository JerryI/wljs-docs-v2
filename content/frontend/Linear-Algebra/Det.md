title: Det
---

`Det[m]` gives the determinant of the square matrix `m`.

## Examples

Compute determinants:

```wolfram
Det[{{1, 2}, {3, 4}}]
(* -2 *)

Det[{{a, b}, {c, d}}]
(* a d - b c *)

(* 3x3 matrix *)
Det[{{1, 2, 3}, {4, 5, 6}, {7, 8, 9}}]
(* 0 *)
```
