---
title: Permanent
---

`Permanent[m]` gives the permanent of the square matrix m.

## Examples

Compute the permanent of a matrix:

```wolfram
Permanent[{{a, b}, {c, d}}]
(* a d + b c *)
```

Permanent of a numeric matrix:

```wolfram
Permanent[{{1, 2}, {3, 4}}]
(* 10 *)
```
