---
title: TreeForm
---

`TreeForm[expr]` displays expr as a tree with different levels at different depths.

## Examples

Visualize expression structure:

```wolfram
TreeForm[a + b*c]
```

Limit depth:

```wolfram
TreeForm[{{1, 2}, {3, 4}}, 2]
```
