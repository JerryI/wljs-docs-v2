---
title: TensorDimensions
---

`TensorDimensions[tensor]` gives the list of dimensions of tensor.

## Examples

Dimensions of a matrix:

```wolfram
TensorDimensions[{{1, 2, 3}, {4, 5, 6}}]
(* {2, 3} *)
```

Dimensions of a 3D array:

```wolfram
TensorDimensions[{{{1, 2}, {3, 4}}, {{5, 6}, {7, 8}}}]
(* {2, 2, 2} *)
```
