---
title: TreeLeaves
---

`TreeLeaves[tree]` returns the list of leaves of the tree.

## Examples

Get the leaves of a tree:

```wolfram
TreeLeaves[Tree[a, {Tree[b, {c, d}], e}]]
(* {c, d, e} *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/TreeLeaves.html) for more details.
