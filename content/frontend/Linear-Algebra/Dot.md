---
title: Dot
---

`a . b` or `Dot[a, b]` gives products of vectors, matrices, and tensors.

## Examples

Vector dot product:

```wolfram
{1, 2, 3} . {4, 5, 6}
(* 32 *)
```

Matrix multiplication:

```wolfram
{{1, 2}, {3, 4}} . {{5, 6}, {7, 8}}
(* {{19, 22}, {43, 50}} *)
```
