---
title: PauliMatrix
---

`PauliMatrix[k]` gives the kth Pauli spin matrix σk.

## Examples

```wolfram
PauliMatrix[1]
```

```wolfram
Table[PauliMatrix[k], {k, 0, 3}]
```
