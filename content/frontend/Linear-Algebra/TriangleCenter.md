---
title: TriangleCenter
---

`TriangleCenter[tri, type]` gives the specified type of center for the triangle tri.

- `TriangleCenter[tri]` gives the centroid of the triangle.

## Examples

```wolfram
TriangleCenter[Triangle[{{0, 0}, {1, 0}, {0, 1}}], "Incenter"]
```
