---
title: HilbertMatrix
---

`HilbertMatrix[n]` gives the n×n Hilbert matrix with elements of the form 1/(i+j-1).

- `HilbertMatrix[{m, n}]` gives the m×n Hilbert matrix.

## Examples

```wolfram
HilbertMatrix[3]
```

```wolfram
HilbertMatrix[{2, 4}] // MatrixForm
```
