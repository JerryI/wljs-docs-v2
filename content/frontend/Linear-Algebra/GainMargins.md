---
title: GainMargins
---

`GainMargins[lsys]` gives the gain margins of the linear time-invariant system lsys.

## Examples

Compute gain margins of a transfer function:

```wolfram
GainMargins[TransferFunctionModel[1/(s^2 + s + 1), s]]
```
