---
title: TensorRank
---

`TensorRank[tensor]` gives the rank of a tensor.

## Examples

Get the rank of a matrix:

```wolfram
TensorRank[{{1, 2}, {3, 4}}]
(* 2 *)
```

Get the rank of a vector:

```wolfram
TensorRank[{1, 2, 3}]
(* 1 *)
```
