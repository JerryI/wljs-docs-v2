---
title: InverseEllipticNomeQ
---

`InverseEllipticNomeQ[q]` gives the parameter `m` corresponding to the nome `q` in an elliptic function.

## Examples

```wolfram
InverseEllipticNomeQ[0.1]
```

```wolfram
InverseEllipticNomeQ[EllipticNomeQ[0.5]]
```
