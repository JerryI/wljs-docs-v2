---
title: HermitianMatrix
---

`HermitianMatrix[hmat]` converts the Hermitian matrix hmat to a structured array.

## Examples

```wolfram
HermitianMatrix[{{1, 2 + I}, {2 - I, 3}}]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/HermitianMatrix.html) for more details.
