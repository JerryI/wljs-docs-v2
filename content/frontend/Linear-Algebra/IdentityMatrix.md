---
title: IdentityMatrix
---

`IdentityMatrix[n]` gives the n×n identity matrix.

`IdentityMatrix[{m, n}]` gives an m×n identity matrix.

## Examples

Create a 3×3 identity matrix:

```wolfram
IdentityMatrix[3]
(* {{1, 0, 0}, {0, 1, 0}, {0, 0, 1}} *)
```

Rectangular identity matrix:

```wolfram
IdentityMatrix[{2, 3}]
(* {{1, 0, 0}, {0, 1, 0}} *)
```
