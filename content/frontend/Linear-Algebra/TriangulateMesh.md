---
title: TriangulateMesh
---

`TriangulateMesh[mr]` generates a triangulation of the mesh region mr.

## Examples

```wolfram
TriangulateMesh[Rectangle[]]
```

```wolfram
TriangulateMesh[Disk[], MaxCellMeasure -> 0.1]
```
