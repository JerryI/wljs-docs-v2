---
title: OuterPolyhedron
---

`OuterPolyhedron[poly]` gives the outer polyhedron of the polyhedron poly.

## Examples

```wolfram
OuterPolyhedron[PolyhedronData["Icosahedron"]]
```

```wolfram
Graphics3D[OuterPolyhedron[PolyhedronData["Dodecahedron"]]]
```
