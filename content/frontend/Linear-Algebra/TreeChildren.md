---
title: TreeChildren
---

`TreeChildren[tree]` extracts the children of the root of the `Tree` object tree.

## Examples

```wolfram
t = Tree[a, {Tree[b, {c, d}], Tree[e, {f}]}];
TreeChildren[t]
```
