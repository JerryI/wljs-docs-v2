---
title: ZeroSymmetric
---

`ZeroSymmetric[{s1, ..., sn}]` represents the symmetry of a zero tensor in the slots si.

## Examples

```wolfram
ZeroSymmetric[{1, 2}]
```

```wolfram
TensorSymmetry[SymmetrizedArray[{}, {3, 3}, ZeroSymmetric[{1, 2}]]]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/ZeroSymmetric.html) for more details.
