---
title: TreeLeafCount
---

`TreeLeafCount[tree]` gives the number of leaves of tree.

## Examples

```wolfram
TreeLeafCount[Tree[a, {Tree[b, None], Tree[c, None]}]]
```

```wolfram
TreeLeafCount[ExpressionTree[a + b + c]]
```
