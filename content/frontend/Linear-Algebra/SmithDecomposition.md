---
title: SmithDecomposition
---

`SmithDecomposition[m]` gives the Smith normal form decomposition of an integer matrix m.

## Examples

```wolfram
SmithDecomposition[{{1, 2}, {3, 4}}]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/SmithDecomposition.html) for more details.
