---
title: Cross
---

`Cross[a, b]` gives the vector cross product of a and b.

## Examples

Cross product of 3D vectors:

```wolfram
Cross[{1, 0, 0}, {0, 1, 0}]
(* {0, 0, 1} *)
```

```wolfram
Cross[{1, 2, 3}, {4, 5, 6}]
(* {-3, 6, -3} *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Cross.html) for more details.
