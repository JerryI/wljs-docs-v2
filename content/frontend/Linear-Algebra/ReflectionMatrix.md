---
title: ReflectionMatrix
---

`ReflectionMatrix[v]` gives the matrix that represents reflection of points in a mirror normal to the vector v.

## Examples

```wolfram
ReflectionMatrix[{1, 0}]
(* {{-1, 0}, {0, 1}} *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/ReflectionMatrix.html) for more details.
