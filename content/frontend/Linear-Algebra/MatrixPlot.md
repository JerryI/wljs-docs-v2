---
title: MatrixPlot
---

`MatrixPlot[m]` generates a plot that gives a visual representation of the values of elements in a matrix.

## Examples

Visualize a matrix:

```wolfram
MatrixPlot[Table[i + j, {i, 10}, {j, 10}]]
```

Identity matrix:

```wolfram
MatrixPlot[IdentityMatrix[10]]
```
