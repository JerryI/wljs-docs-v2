---
title: InverseRadonTransform
---

`InverseRadonTransform[expr, {p, ϕ}, {x, y}]` gives the inverse Radon transform of expr.

## Examples

```wolfram
InverseRadonTransform[DiracDelta[p], {p, ϕ}, {x, y}]
```

```wolfram
InverseRadonTransform[Exp[-p^2], {p, ϕ}, {x, y}]
```
