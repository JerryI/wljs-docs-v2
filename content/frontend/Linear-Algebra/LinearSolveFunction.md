---
title: LinearSolveFunction
---

`LinearSolveFunction[dimensions, data]` represents a function for providing solutions to a matrix equation.

## Examples

```wolfram
lsf = LinearSolve[{{1, 2}, {3, 4}}];
lsf[{5, 6}]
```
