---
title: DrazinInverse
---

`DrazinInverse[m]` finds the Drazin generalized inverse of a square matrix m.

## Examples

```wolfram
DrazinInverse[{{1, 2}, {0, 0}}]
(* {{1, 0}, {0, 0}} *)
```
