---
title: HermiteDecomposition
---

`HermiteDecomposition[m]` gives the Hermite normal form decomposition of an integer matrix m.

## Examples

```wolfram
HermiteDecomposition[{{1, 2}, {3, 4}}]
```

```wolfram
HermiteDecomposition[{{2, 4, 6}, {1, 3, 5}, {0, 2, 4}}]
```
