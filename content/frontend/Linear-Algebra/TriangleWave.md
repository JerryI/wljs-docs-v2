---
title: TriangleWave
---

`TriangleWave[x]` gives a triangle wave that varies between -1 and +1 with unit period.

- `TriangleWave[{min, max}, x]` gives a triangle wave that varies between min and max with unit period.

## Examples

```wolfram
Plot[TriangleWave[x], {x, 0, 3}]
```
