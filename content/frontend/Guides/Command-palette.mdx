WLJS Notebook is fully accessible from the keyboard. One of the most important key combination to know is <kbd>Ctrl-P</kbd> or <kbd>⌘-P</kbd>, which brings up the Command Palette. From here, you have access to many small helper utilities, toolboxes, shortcuts and AI copilot:

## Shortcuts
Type any of those words to call the action:

<Card>

- __New notebook__ - create a new notebook (in a new window)
- __New notebook from template__ - create a new notebook from a template
- __Rename notebook__ - rename current notebook
- __Debug__ - attach a debugger
- __Print__ - export an entire notebook to PDF

<Callout>Notebooks are heavily cached to avoid excessive writting on a disk, therefore avoid renaming an opened notebook outside the WLJS application</Callout>

</Card>
