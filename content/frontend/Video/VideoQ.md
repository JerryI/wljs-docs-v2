---
title: VideoQ
---

`VideoQ[video]` yields `True` if video has the form of a valid `Video` object, and `False` otherwise.

## Examples

```wolfram
VideoQ[Video["test.mp4"]]
```

```wolfram
VideoQ["not a video"]
```
