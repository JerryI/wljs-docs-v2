---
title: VideoJoin
---

`VideoJoin[video1, video2, ...]` concatenates all videoi and returns a video object.

## Examples

Concatenate two videos:
```wolfram
VideoJoin[video1, video2]
```

Join multiple video clips:
```wolfram
VideoJoin[clip1, clip2, clip3, clip4]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/VideoJoin.html) for more details.
