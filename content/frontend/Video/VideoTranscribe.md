---
title: VideoTranscribe
---

`VideoTranscribe[video]` recognizes speech in an audio track and adds it to video as a subtitle track.

## Examples

```wolfram
VideoTranscribe[Video["speech.mp4"]]
```

```wolfram
VideoTranscribe[video, Language -> "French"]
```
