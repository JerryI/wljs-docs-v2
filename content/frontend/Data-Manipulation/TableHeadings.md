---
title: TableHeadings
---

`TableHeadings` is an option for `TableForm` and `MatrixForm` that gives the labels to be printed for entries in each dimension of a table or matrix.

## Examples

```wolfram
TableForm[{{1, 2}, {3, 4}}, TableHeadings -> {{"Row1", "Row2"}, {"Col1", "Col2"}}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/TableHeadings.html) for more details.*
