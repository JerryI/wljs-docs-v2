---
title: LanczosWindow
---

`LanczosWindow[x]` represents a Lanczos window function of x.

## Examples

Evaluate the Lanczos window at a point:

```wolfram
LanczosWindow[0]
(* 1 *)
```

Plot the window function:

```wolfram
Plot[LanczosWindow[x], {x, -1, 1}]
```
