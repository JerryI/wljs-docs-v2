---
title: MeanGraphDistance
---

`MeanGraphDistance[g]` gives the mean distance between all pairs of vertices in the graph g.

- `MeanGraphDistance[{v -> w, …}]` uses rules v -> w to specify the graph g.

## Examples

```wolfram
g = Graph[{1 <-> 2, 2 <-> 3, 3 <-> 4}];
MeanGraphDistance[g]
```
