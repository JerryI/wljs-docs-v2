---
title: Identity
---

`Identity[expr]` gives expr (the identity operation).

## Examples

Identity function:

```wolfram
Identity[5]
(* 5 *)

Identity[{a, b, c}]
(* {a, b, c} *)
```

Useful with Map:

```wolfram
Map[Identity, {1, 2, 3}]
(* {1, 2, 3} *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Identity.html) for more details.
