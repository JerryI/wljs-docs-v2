---
title: Join
---

`Join[list1, list2, ...]` concatenates lists or other expressions that share the same head.

## Examples

Join two lists:

```wolfram
Join[{a, b}, {c, d}]
(* {a, b, c, d} *)
```

Join multiple lists:

```wolfram
Join[{1, 2}, {3, 4}, {5, 6}]
(* {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6} *)
```
