---
title: PartitionsQ
---

`PartitionsQ[n]` gives the number q(n) of partitions of the integer n into distinct parts.

## Examples

Distinct partitions:

```wolfram
PartitionsQ[5]
(* 3 *)
```

Compare with PartitionsP:

```wolfram
{PartitionsP[10], PartitionsQ[10]}
(* {42, 10} *)
```
