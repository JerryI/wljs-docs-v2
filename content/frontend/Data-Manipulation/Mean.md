---
title: Mean
---

`Mean[data]` gives the arithmetic mean of the elements in data.

`Mean[dist]` gives the mean of the distribution dist.

## Examples

Mean of a list:

```wolfram
Mean[{1, 2, 3, 4, 5}]
(* 3 *)
```

Mean of a distribution:

```wolfram
Mean[NormalDistribution[0, 1]]
(* 0 *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Mean.html) for more details.
