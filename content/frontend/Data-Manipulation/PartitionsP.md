---
title: PartitionsP
---

`PartitionsP[n]` gives the number p(n) of unrestricted partitions of the integer n.

## Examples

Number of partitions:

```wolfram
PartitionsP[5]
(* 7 *)
```

Larger value:

```wolfram
PartitionsP[100]
(* 190569292 *)
```
