---
title: Total
---

`Total[list]` gives the total of the elements in list.

`Total[list, n]` totals all elements down to level n.

## Examples

Sum a list:

```wolfram
Total[{1, 2, 3, 4, 5}]
(* 15 *)
```

Total of nested lists:

```wolfram
Total[{{1, 2}, {3, 4}}, 2]
(* 10 *)
```
