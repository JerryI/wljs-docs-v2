---
title: RotateLeft
---

`RotateLeft[expr, n]` cycles the elements in expr n positions to the left.

`RotateLeft[expr]` cycles one position to the left.

## Examples

Rotate left by 1:

```wolfram
RotateLeft[{a, b, c, d, e}]
(* {b, c, d, e, a} *)
```

Rotate left by 2:

```wolfram
RotateLeft[{a, b, c, d, e}, 2]
(* {c, d, e, a, b} *)
```
