# ArcSinDegrees

`ArcSinDegrees[z]` gives the arc sine in degrees of the complex number z.

## Examples

Get arc sine in degrees:

```wolfram
ArcSinDegrees[0.5]
(* 30. *)
```

Exact value:

```wolfram
ArcSinDegrees[1]
(* 90 *)
```
