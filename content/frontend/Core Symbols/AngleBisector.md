# AngleBisector

```
AngleBisector[{q1,p,q2}] gives the bisector of the interior angle at p formed by the triangle with vertex points p, q1 and q2.

AngleBisector[{q1,p,q2},"type"] gives the angle bisector of the specified type.
```
