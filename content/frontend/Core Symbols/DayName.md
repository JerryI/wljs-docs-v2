# DayName

`DayName[]` gives the current day of the week.

`DayName[date]` gives the day of the week for the given date.

## Examples

Get current day:

```wolfram
DayName[]
(* Wednesday *)
```

Day of a specific date:

```wolfram
DayName[{2024, 12, 25}]
(* Wednesday *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/DayName.html) for more details.*
