# FullMoon

`FullMoon[]` gives the date of the next full moon.

- `FullMoon[date]` gives the date of the first full moon after the given date.

## Examples

Get the date of the next full moon:

```wolfram
FullMoon[]
```

Find the full moon after a specific date:

```wolfram
FullMoon[DateObject[{2024, 6, 1}]]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/FullMoon.html) for more details.*
