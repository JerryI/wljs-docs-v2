# ArcCsch

`ArcCsch[z]` gives the inverse hyperbolic cosecant csch⁻¹(z) of the complex number z.

## Examples

Evaluate the inverse hyperbolic cosecant:

```wolfram
ArcCsch[1]
(* Log[1 + Sqrt[2]] *)
```

Numerical value:

```wolfram
N[ArcCsch[2]]
(* 0.481212 *)
```
