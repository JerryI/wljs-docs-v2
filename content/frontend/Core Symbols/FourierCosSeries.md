# FourierCosSeries

```
FourierCosSeries[expr,t,n] gives the nth-order Fourier cosine series expansion of expr in t.

FourierCosSeries[expr,{t1,t2,…},{n1,n2,…}] gives the multidimensional Fourier cosine series of expr.
```
