# FourierCoefficient

```
FourierCoefficient[expr,t,n] gives the nth coefficient in the Fourier series expansion of expr.

FourierCoefficient[expr,{t1,t2,…},{n1,n2,…}] gives a multidimensional Fourier coefficient.
```
