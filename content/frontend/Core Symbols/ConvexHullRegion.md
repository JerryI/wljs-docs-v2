# ConvexHullRegion

`ConvexHullRegion[{p1,p2,…}]` gives the convex hull from the points p1, p2, ….

`ConvexHullRegion[reg]` gives the convex hull of the region reg.

## Examples

```wolfram
ConvexHullRegion[{{0,0}, {1,0}, {0,1}, {1,1}, {0.5,0.5}}]
(* Polygon[{{0,0}, {1,0}, {1,1}, {0,1}}] *)
```
