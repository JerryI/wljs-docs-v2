# BellY

```
BellY[n,k,{x1,…,xn-k+1}] gives the partial Bell polynomial Yn,k(x1,…,xn-k+1).

BellY[n,k,m] gives the generalized partial Bell polynomial of a matrix m.

BellY[m] gives the generalized Bell polynomial of a matrix m.
```
