# FourierCosTransform

```
FourierCosTransform[expr,t,ω] gives the symbolic Fourier cosine transform of expr. 

FourierCosTransform[expr,{t1,t2,…},{ω1,ω2,…}] gives the multidimensional Fourier cosine transform of expr. 
```
