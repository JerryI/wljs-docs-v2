# AsymptoticLess

```
AsymptoticLess[f,g,x->x*] gives conditions for f(x)≺g(x) or f(x)∈o(g(x)) as x->x*.

AsymptoticLess[f,g,{x1,…,xn}->{x1*,…,xn*}] gives conditions for f(x1,…,xn)≺g(x1,…,xn) or f(x1,…,xn)∈o(g(x1,…,xn)) as {x1,…,xn}->{x1*,…,xn*}.
```
