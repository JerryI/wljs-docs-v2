---
title: ConstantPlusLayer
---

`ConstantPlusLayer[]` represents a layer that adds a learnable bias to its input.

`ConstantPlusLayer[opts]` includes options for initial bias and other parameters.

## Examples

Create a bias layer:

```wolfram
ConstantPlusLayer[]
```
