---
title: NetMapOperator
---

`NetMapOperator[net]` represents a net in which net is mapped over a sequence of inputs to give a sequence of outputs.

## Examples

```wolfram
NetMapOperator[LinearLayer[10]]
```

```wolfram
NetMapOperator[ElementwiseLayer["ReLU"]]
```
