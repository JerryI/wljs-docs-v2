---
title: DotLayer
---

`DotLayer[]` represents a net layer that takes the dot product of two or more arrays.

`DotLayer[{spec1, spec2, ...}]` uses given transpose specifications for the respective inputs.

## Examples

Create a dot product layer:

```wolfram
DotLayer[]
```
