---
title: IntegerPart
---

`IntegerPart[x]` gives the integer part of x.

## Examples

Integer part of positive:

```wolfram
IntegerPart[3.7]
(* 3 *)
```

Integer part of negative:

```wolfram
IntegerPart[-3.7]
(* -3 *)
```
