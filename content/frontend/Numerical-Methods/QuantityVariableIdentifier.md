---
title: QuantityVariableIdentifier
---

`QuantityVariableIdentifier[quantityvariable]` returns the identifier associated with the specified *quantityvariable*.

## Examples

```wolfram
QuantityVariableIdentifier[QuantityVariable["t", "Time"]]
```
