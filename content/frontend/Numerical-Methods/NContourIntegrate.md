---
title: NContourIntegrate
---

`NContourIntegrate[f, z ∈ cont]` gives the numerical integral of f along the contour defined by cont in the complex plane.

## Examples

```wolfram
NContourIntegrate[1/z, z \[Element] Circle[]]
```
