---
title: FractionalPart
---

`FractionalPart[x]` gives the fractional part of x.

## Examples

Fractional part of positive number:

```wolfram
FractionalPart[3.7]
(* 0.7 *)
```

Fractional part of negative number:

```wolfram
FractionalPart[-2.3]
(* -0.3 *)
```
