---
title: Accuracy
---

`Accuracy[x]` gives the effective number of digits to the right of the decimal point in the number x.

## Examples

```wolfram
Accuracy[3.14159265358979]
(* 14.5086 *)
```

```wolfram
Accuracy[N[Pi, 50]]
(* 49.5086 *)
```
