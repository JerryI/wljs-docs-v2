---
title: UnitVector
---

`UnitVector[n, k]` gives the n-dimensional unit vector in the kth direction.

`UnitVector[k]` gives a 2D unit vector in the kth direction.

## Examples

2D unit vectors:

```wolfram
UnitVector[1]
(* {1, 0} *)

UnitVector[2]
(* {0, 1} *)
```

3D unit vector:

```wolfram
UnitVector[3, 2]
(* {0, 1, 0} *)
```
