---
title: UnitaryMatrix
---

`UnitaryMatrix[umat]` converts the unitary matrix umat to a structured array.

## Examples

Create a unitary matrix:

```wolfram
UnitaryMatrix[{{1, 0}, {0, 1}}]
```
