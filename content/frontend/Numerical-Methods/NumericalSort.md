---
title: NumericalSort
---

`NumericalSort[list]` sorts the elements of list into numerical order.

## Examples

```wolfram
NumericalSort[{3, 1, 4, 1, 5, 9, 2, 6}]
```

```wolfram
NumericalSort[{"10", "2", "1"}]
```
