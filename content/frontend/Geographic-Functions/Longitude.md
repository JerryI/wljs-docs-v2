---
title: Longitude
---

`Longitude[pos]` gives the longitude in degrees of a geographic position specified by pos.

`Longitude[pos,datum]` gives the longitude referring to the specified geodetic datum.

## Examples

```wolfram
Longitude[Entity["City", {"London", "GreaterLondon", "UnitedKingdom"}]]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Longitude.html) for more details.
