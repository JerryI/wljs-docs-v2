## Where the money goes

These are our approximate annual costs and budget:

| Expense | Annual budget |
| --- | ---: |
| Apple Developer membership | €100 |
| Domain name | €100 |
| Occasional CDN edge requests, cloud credits for testing, and coffee | €100 |
| **Total** | **€300** |

We work on WLJS alongside our research and other commitments. Your support helps cover these project expenses and the occasional coffee while we fix bugs and write tutorials.
