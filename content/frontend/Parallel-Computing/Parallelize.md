---
title: Parallelize
---

`Parallelize[expr]` evaluates expr using automatic parallelization.

## Examples

```wolfram
Parallelize[Table[i^2, {i, 10}]]
(* {1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100} *)
```
