---
title: PolyhedronCoordinates
---

`PolyhedronCoordinates[poly]` gives a list of coordinates in the polyhedron poly.

## Examples

Get the vertex coordinates of a tetrahedron:

```wolfram
PolyhedronCoordinates[Tetrahedron[]]
```
