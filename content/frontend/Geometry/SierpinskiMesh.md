---
title: SierpinskiMesh
---

`SierpinskiMesh[n]` gives a mesh region representing the nth-step Sierpiński triangle.

- `SierpinskiMesh[n, d]` gives the nth-step Sierpiński sponge in dimension d.

## Examples

Create a Sierpiński triangle:

```wolfram
SierpinskiMesh[4]
```

3D Sierpiński sponge (Menger sponge):

```wolfram
SierpinskiMesh[2, 3]
```

Higher resolution:

```wolfram
SierpinskiMesh[6]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/SierpinskiMesh.html) for more details.
