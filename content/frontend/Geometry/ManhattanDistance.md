---
title: ManhattanDistance
---

`ManhattanDistance[u, v]` gives the Manhattan or "city block" distance between vectors u and v.

## Examples

```wolfram
ManhattanDistance[{0, 0}, {3, 4}]
(* 7 *)
```

```wolfram
ManhattanDistance[{1, 2, 3}, {4, 5, 6}]
(* 9 *)
```
