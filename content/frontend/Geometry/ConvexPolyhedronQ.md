---
title: ConvexPolyhedronQ
---

`ConvexPolyhedronQ[poly]` gives True if the polyhedron poly is convex, and False otherwise.

## Examples

Check if a polyhedron is convex:

```wolfram
ConvexPolyhedronQ[Cuboid[]]
(* True *)
```
