---
title: InscribedBall
---

`InscribedBall[{p1,p2,…}]` gives the largest ball that lies inside the convex hull of the points p1,p2,….

## Examples

Find the inscribed ball of a set of points:

```wolfram
pts = {{0, 0}, {1, 0}, {0, 1}, {1, 1}};
InscribedBall[pts]
```
