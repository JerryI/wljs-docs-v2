---
title: EuclideanDistance
---

`EuclideanDistance[u, v]` gives the Euclidean distance between vectors u and v.

## Examples

Calculate the distance between two points:

```wolfram
EuclideanDistance[{0, 0}, {3, 4}]
(* 5 *)
```

Distance in 3D:

```wolfram
EuclideanDistance[{1, 2, 3}, {4, 5, 6}]
(* 3 Sqrt[3] *)
```
