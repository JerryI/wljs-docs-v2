---
title: RegionMeasure
---

`RegionMeasure[reg]` gives the measure (length, area, volume) of the region.

## Examples

Area of disk:

```wolfram
RegionMeasure[Disk[]]
(* Pi *)
```

Volume of ball:

```wolfram
RegionMeasure[Ball[]]
(* (4 Pi)/3 *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/RegionMeasure.html) for more details.
