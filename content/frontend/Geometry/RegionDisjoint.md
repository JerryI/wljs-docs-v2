---
title: RegionDisjoint
---

`RegionDisjoint[reg1, reg2]` returns `True` if the regions reg1 and reg2 are disjoint.

- `RegionDisjoint[reg1, reg2, reg3, …]` returns `True` if the regions reg1, reg2, reg3, … are pairwise disjoint.

## Examples

```wolfram
RegionDisjoint[Disk[{0, 0}], Disk[{5, 5}]]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/RegionDisjoint.html) for more details.*
