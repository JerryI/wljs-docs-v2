---
title: MengerMesh
---

`MengerMesh[n]` gives a mesh region representing the nth-step Menger sponge.

- `MengerMesh[n, d]` gives the nth-step Menger sponge in dimension d.

## Examples

Create a Menger sponge:
```wolfram
MengerMesh[2]
```

2D Sierpiński carpet:
```wolfram
MengerMesh[4, 2]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/MengerMesh.html) for more details.
