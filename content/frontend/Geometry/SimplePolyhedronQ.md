---
title: SimplePolyhedronQ
---

`SimplePolyhedronQ[poly]` gives `True` if the polyhedron poly is simple and `False` otherwise.

## Examples

```wolfram
SimplePolyhedronQ[Tetrahedron[]]
```

```wolfram
SimplePolyhedronQ[Cuboid[]]
```
