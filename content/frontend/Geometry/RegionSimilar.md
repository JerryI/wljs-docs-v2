---
title: RegionSimilar
---

`RegionSimilar[reg1, reg2]` tests whether the regions reg1 and reg2 are similar.

## Examples

```wolfram
RegionSimilar[Disk[], Disk[{0, 0}, 2]]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/RegionSimilar.html) for more details.
