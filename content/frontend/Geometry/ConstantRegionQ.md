---
title: ConstantRegionQ
---

`ConstantRegionQ[reg]` gives `True` if the reg is a constant region and `False` otherwise.

## Examples

```wolfram
ConstantRegionQ[Disk[]]
(* True *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/ConstantRegionQ.html) for more details.*
