---
title: SASTriangle
---

`SASTriangle[a, γ, b]` returns a filled triangle with sides of length a and b and angle γ between them.

## Examples

```wolfram
Graphics[SASTriangle[1, Pi/3, 1.5]]
```

```wolfram
SASTriangle[2, Pi/4, 3]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/SASTriangle.html) for more details.
