---
title: RegionEmbeddingDimension
---

`RegionEmbeddingDimension[reg]` gives the dimension of the space in which the region reg is embedded.

## Examples

Get the embedding dimension of a disk:

```wolfram
RegionEmbeddingDimension[Disk[]]
(* 2 *)
```
