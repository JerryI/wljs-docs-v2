---
title: SpatialObservationRegionQ
---

`SpatialObservationRegionQ[reg]` tests whether the geometric or geographic region *reg* can be an observation in spatial statistics.

## Examples

```wolfram
SpatialObservationRegionQ[Disk[]]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/SpatialObservationRegionQ.html) for more details.
