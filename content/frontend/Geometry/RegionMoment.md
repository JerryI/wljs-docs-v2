---
title: RegionMoment
---

`RegionMoment[reg, {i1, i2, ..., in}]` computes the polynomial moment for the region reg.

## Examples

Compute the zeroth moment (area) of a disk:

```wolfram
RegionMoment[Disk[], {0, 0}]
(* Pi *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/RegionMoment.html) for more details.
