---
title: CircumscribedBall
---

`CircumscribedBall[{p1, p2, ...}]` gives a ball with minimal radius that encloses the points p1, p2, ....

## Examples

Find the smallest ball enclosing points:

```wolfram
CircumscribedBall[{{0, 0}, {1, 0}, {0, 1}}]
```
