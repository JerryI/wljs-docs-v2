---
title: ConvexPolygonQ
---

`ConvexPolygonQ[poly]` gives True if the polygon poly is convex, and False otherwise.

## Examples

Check if a polygon is convex:

```wolfram
ConvexPolygonQ[Polygon[{{0, 0}, {1, 0}, {1, 1}, {0, 1}}]]
(* True *)
```
