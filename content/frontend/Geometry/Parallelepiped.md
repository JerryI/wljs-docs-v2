---
title: Parallelepiped
---

`Parallelepiped[p, {v1, ..., vk}]` represents a parallelepiped with origin p and edge directions vi.

## Examples

3D parallelepiped:

```wolfram
Graphics3D[Parallelepiped[{0, 0, 0}, {{1, 0, 0}, {0.5, 1, 0}, {0, 0.5, 1}}]]
```

2D parallelogram:

```wolfram
Graphics[Parallelepiped[{0, 0}, {{1, 0}, {0.5, 1}}]]
```
