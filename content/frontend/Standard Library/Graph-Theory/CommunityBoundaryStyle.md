---
title: CommunityBoundaryStyle
---

`CommunityBoundaryStyle` is an option to `CommunityGraphPlot` that specifies how to style community boundaries.

## Examples

Style community boundaries:

```wolfram
CommunityGraphPlot[graph, CommunityBoundaryStyle -> Dashed]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/CommunityBoundaryStyle.html) for more details.*
