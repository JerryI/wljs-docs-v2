---
title: Times
---

`x * y * z` or `x y z` represents a product of terms.

## Examples

Arithmetic multiplication:

```wolfram
2 * 3 * 4
(* 24 *)
```

Symbolic multiplication:

```wolfram
a b c
(* a b c *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Times.html) for more details.*
