---
title: BarnesG
---

`BarnesG[z]` gives the Barnes G-function G(z).

## Examples

Compute the Barnes G-function:

```wolfram
BarnesG[5]
(* 288 *)
```

```wolfram
N[BarnesG[2.5]]
```
