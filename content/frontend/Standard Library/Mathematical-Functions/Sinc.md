---
title: Sinc
---

`Sinc[z]` gives sinc(z).

## Examples

Compute the sinc function:

```wolfram
Sinc[Pi]
(* 0 *)
```

Plot the sinc function:

```wolfram
Plot[Sinc[x], {x, -10, 10}]
```
