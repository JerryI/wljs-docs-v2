---
title: EllipticReducedHalfPeriods
---

`EllipticReducedHalfPeriods[{u, v}]` gives a reduced pair of half periods {w, w'} corresponding to the same lattice as that of {u, v}.

## Examples

Get reduced half periods:

```wolfram
EllipticReducedHalfPeriods[{1, I}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/EllipticReducedHalfPeriods.html) for more details.*
