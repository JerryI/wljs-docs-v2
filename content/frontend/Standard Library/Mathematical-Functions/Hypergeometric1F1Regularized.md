---
title: Hypergeometric1F1Regularized
---

`Hypergeometric1F1Regularized[a, b, z]` is the regularized confluent hypergeometric function ₁F₁(a; b; z)/Γ(b).

## Examples

```wolfram
Hypergeometric1F1Regularized[1, 2, 1]
```
