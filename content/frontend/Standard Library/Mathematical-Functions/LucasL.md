---
title: LucasL
---

`LucasL[n]` gives the Lucas number Ln.

`LucasL[n,x]` gives the Lucas polynomial Ln(x).

## Examples

Compute the 10th Lucas number:

```wolfram
LucasL[10]
(* 123 *)
```

Get a Lucas polynomial:

```wolfram
LucasL[4, x]
(* 2 + 4 x^2 + x^4 *)
```
