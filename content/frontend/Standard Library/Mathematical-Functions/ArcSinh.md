---
title: ArcSinh
---

`ArcSinh[z]` gives the inverse hyperbolic sine sinh⁻¹(z) of the complex number z.

## Examples

Evaluate the inverse hyperbolic sine:

```wolfram
ArcSinh[1]
(* Log[1 + Sqrt[2]] *)
```

Numerical value:

```wolfram
N[ArcSinh[2]]
(* 1.44364 *)
```
