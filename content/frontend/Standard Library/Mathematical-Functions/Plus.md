---
title: Plus
---

`x + y + z` represents a sum of terms.

## Examples

Arithmetic addition:

```wolfram
2 + 3 + 5
(* 10 *)
```

Symbolic addition:

```wolfram
a + b + a
(* 2a + b *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Plus.html) for more details.*
