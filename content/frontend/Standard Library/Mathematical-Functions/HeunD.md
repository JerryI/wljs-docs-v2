---
title: HeunD
---

`HeunD[q, α, γ, δ, ϵ, z]` gives the double-confluent Heun function.

## Examples

Evaluate at a point:

```wolfram
HeunD[1, 2, 0.5, 0.5, 0.5, 0.3]
```

Symbolic form:

```wolfram
HeunD[q, a, g, d, e, z]
```

Plot the function:

```wolfram
Plot[HeunD[1, 1, 0.5, 0.5, 0.5, x], {x, 0, 2}]
```
