---
title: AppellF1
---

`AppellF1[a, b1, b2, c, x, y]` is the Appell hypergeometric function of two variables F1(a; b1, b2; c; x, y).

## Examples

Evaluate the Appell F1 function:

```wolfram
AppellF1[1, 2, 3, 4, 0.5, 0.3]
```

Symbolic evaluation:

```wolfram
AppellF1[a, b1, b2, c, 0, 0]
(* 1 *)
```
