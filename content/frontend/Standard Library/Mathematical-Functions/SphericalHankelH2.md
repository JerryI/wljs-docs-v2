---
title: SphericalHankelH2
---

`SphericalHankelH2[n, z]` gives the spherical Hankel function of the second kind h_n^(2)(z).

## Examples

```wolfram
SphericalHankelH2[0, 1.0]
```

```wolfram
SphericalHankelH2[2, x]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/SphericalHankelH2.html) for more details.*
