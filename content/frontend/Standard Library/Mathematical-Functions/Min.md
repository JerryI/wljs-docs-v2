# Min

`Min[x1, x2, ...]` yields the numerically smallest of the xi.

`Min[{x1, x2, ...}, {y1, ...}, ...]` yields the smallest element of any of the lists.

## Examples

Minimum of numbers:

```wolfram
Min[3, 1, 4, 1, 5, 9]
(* 1 *)
```

Minimum of a list:

```wolfram
Min[{-5, 3, 7, -2}]
(* -5 *)
```
