---
title: DirichletL
---

`DirichletL[k, j, s]` gives the Dirichlet L-function L(χ, s) for the Dirichlet character χ(n) with modulus k and index j.

## Examples

Evaluate L-function:

```wolfram
DirichletL[4, 2, 1]
(* Pi/4 *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
N[DirichletL[5, 2, 2]]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/DirichletL.html) for more details.*
