---
title: HermiteH
---

`HermiteH[n, x]` gives the Hermite polynomial H_n(x), used in quantum mechanics and probability.

## Examples

Hermite polynomials:

```wolfram
HermiteH[0, x]
(* 1 *)

HermiteH[2, x]
(* -2 + 4*x^2 *)

HermiteH[3, x]
(* -12*x + 8*x^3 *)
```

Numerical value:

```wolfram
HermiteH[4, 2]
(* 76 *)
```
