---
title: ArcCot
---

`ArcCot[z]` gives the arc cotangent cot⁻¹(z) of the complex number z.

## Examples

Compute the arc cotangent:

```wolfram
ArcCot[1]
(* Pi/4 *)
```

Numerical evaluation:

```wolfram
ArcCot[2.0]
(* 0.463648 *)
```
