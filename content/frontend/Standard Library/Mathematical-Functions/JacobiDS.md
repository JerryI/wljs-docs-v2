---
title: JacobiDS
---

`JacobiDS[u, m]` gives the Jacobi elliptic function ds(u|m).

## Examples

```wolfram
JacobiDS[0, 0.5]
(* ComplexInfinity *)
```

```wolfram
N[JacobiDS[1, 0.5]]
(* 1.02881 *)
```
