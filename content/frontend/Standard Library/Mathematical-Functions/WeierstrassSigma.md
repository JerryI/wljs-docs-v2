---
title: WeierstrassSigma
---

`WeierstrassSigma[u,{g2,g3}]` gives the Weierstrass sigma function σ(u;g2,g3).

## Examples

Compute the Weierstrass sigma function:

```wolfram
WeierstrassSigma[0.5, {1, 2}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/WeierstrassSigma.html) for more details.*
