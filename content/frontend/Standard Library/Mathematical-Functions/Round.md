# Round

`Round[x]` gives the integer closest to x.

`Round[x, a]` rounds to the nearest multiple of a.

## Examples

Round a number:

```wolfram
Round[3.7]
(* 4 *)

Round[3.2]
(* 3 *)
```

Round to a multiple:

```wolfram
Round[17, 5]
(* 15 *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Round.html) for more details.*
