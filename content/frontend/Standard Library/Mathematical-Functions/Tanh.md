---
title: Tanh
---

`Tanh[z]` gives the hyperbolic tangent of `z`, defined as $\sinh(z)/\cosh(z)$.

## Examples

Compute hyperbolic tangent values:

```wolfram
Tanh[0]
(* 0 *)

Tanh[1.0]
(* 0.761594 *)

(* Tanh approaches 1 for large positive values *)
Tanh[10.0]
(* 0.999999996 *)
```
