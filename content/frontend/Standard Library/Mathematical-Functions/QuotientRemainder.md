---
title: QuotientRemainder
---

`QuotientRemainder[m, n]` gives a list of the quotient and remainder from division of m by n.

## Examples

Get quotient and remainder:

```wolfram
QuotientRemainder[17, 5]
(* {3, 2} *)
```

This means 17 = 5 × 3 + 2.

```wolfram
QuotientRemainder[100, 7]
(* {14, 2} *)
```
