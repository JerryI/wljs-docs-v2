---
title: Signature
---

`Signature[list]` gives the signature of the permutation needed to place the elements of list in canonical order.

## Examples

Signature of an even permutation:

```wolfram
Signature[{1, 2, 3}]
(* 1 *)
```

Signature of an odd permutation:

```wolfram
Signature[{2, 1, 3}]
(* -1 *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Signature.html) for more details.*
