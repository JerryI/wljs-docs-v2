# Log

`Log[z]` gives the natural logarithm of z (logarithm to base e).

`Log[b, z]` gives the logarithm to base b.

## Examples

Natural logarithm:

```wolfram
Log[E]
(* 1 *)

Log[E^2]
(* 2 *)
```

Logarithm with a base:

```wolfram
Log[10, 1000]
(* 3 *)
```
