---
title: HeunCPrime
---

`HeunCPrime[q,α,γ,δ,ϵ,z]` gives the z-derivative of the HeunC function.

## Examples

Evaluate the derivative of HeunC:

```wolfram
HeunCPrime[1, 2, 1, 1, 1, 0.5]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/HeunCPrime.html) for more details.*
