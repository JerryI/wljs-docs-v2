---
title: MathieuGroupM11
---

`MathieuGroupM11[]` represents the sporadic simple Mathieu group M11.

## Examples

Get the order of the Mathieu group M11:

```wolfram
GroupOrder[MathieuGroupM11[]]
(* 7920 *)
```
