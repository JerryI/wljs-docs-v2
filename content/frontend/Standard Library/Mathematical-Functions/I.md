---
title: I
---

`I` represents the imaginary unit $\sqrt{-1}$.

## Examples

Use in complex numbers:

```wolfram
(2 + 3 I) * (1 - I)
(* 5 + I *)
```

Square of I:

```wolfram
I^2
(* -1 *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/I.html) for more details.*
