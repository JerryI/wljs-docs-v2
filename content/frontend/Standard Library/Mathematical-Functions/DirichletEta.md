---
title: DirichletEta
---

`DirichletEta[s]` gives the Dirichlet eta function η(s).

## Examples

Evaluate at specific points:

```wolfram
DirichletEta[2]
(* Pi^2/12 *)
```

Related to Riemann zeta:

```wolfram
DirichletEta[s] == (1 - 2^(1 - s)) Zeta[s]
```
