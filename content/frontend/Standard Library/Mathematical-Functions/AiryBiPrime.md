---
title: AiryBiPrime
---

`AiryBiPrime[z]` gives the derivative of the Airy function Bi'(z).

## Examples

Evaluate the derivative of AiryBi:

```wolfram
AiryBiPrime[0]
(* 3^(1/6)/Gamma[1/3] *)
```

Plot the derivative:

```wolfram
Plot[AiryBiPrime[x], {x, -10, 2}]
```
