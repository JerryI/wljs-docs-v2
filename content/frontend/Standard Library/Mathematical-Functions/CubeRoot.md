# CubeRoot

`CubeRoot[x]` gives the real-valued cube root of x.

## Examples

Compute the cube root of a positive number:

```wolfram
CubeRoot[8]
(* 2 *)
```

Compute the cube root of a negative number:

```wolfram
CubeRoot[-27]
(* -3 *)
```
