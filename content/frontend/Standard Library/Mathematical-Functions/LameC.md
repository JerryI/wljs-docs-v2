---
title: LameC
---

`LameC[ν, j, z, m]` gives the jth Lamé function Ec_ν^(j)(z|m) of order ν with elliptic parameter m.

## Examples

```wolfram
LameC[2, 1, 0.5, 0.3]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/LameC.html) for more details.*
