---
title: TraceOriginal
---

`TraceOriginal` is an option for `Trace` that specifies whether to test the form of each expression before its head and arguments are evaluated.

## Examples

```wolfram
Trace[f[g[x]], TraceOriginal -> True]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/TraceOriginal.html) for more details.*
