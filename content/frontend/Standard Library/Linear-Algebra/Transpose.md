---
title: Transpose
---

`Transpose[list]` transposes the first two levels in list.

## Examples

Transpose a matrix:

```wolfram
Transpose[{{1, 2, 3}, {4, 5, 6}}]
(* {{1, 4}, {2, 5}, {3, 6}} *)
```

Pairs to lists:

```wolfram
Transpose[{{a, 1}, {b, 2}, {c, 3}}]
(* {{a, b, c}, {1, 2, 3}} *)
```
