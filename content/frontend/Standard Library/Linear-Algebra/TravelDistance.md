---
title: TravelDistance
---

`TravelDistance[{loc1,loc2,…}]` gives the estimated distance for travel from loc1 to loc2, ….

## Examples

Find the travel distance between two cities:

```wolfram
TravelDistance[{Entity["City", {"NewYork", "NewYork", "UnitedStates"}], Entity["City", {"Boston", "Massachusetts", "UnitedStates"}]}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/TravelDistance.html) for more details.*
