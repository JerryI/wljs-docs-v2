---
title: InverseJacobiDS
---

`InverseJacobiDS[v, m]` gives the inverse Jacobi elliptic function ds⁻¹(v|m).

## Examples

Evaluate the inverse Jacobi DS function:

```wolfram
InverseJacobiDS[2.0, 0.5]
(* 0.481212 *)
```

Symbolic form:

```wolfram
InverseJacobiDS[v, m]
(* InverseJacobiDS[v, m] *)
```
