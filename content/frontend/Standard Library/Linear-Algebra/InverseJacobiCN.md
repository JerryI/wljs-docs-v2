---
title: InverseJacobiCN
---

`InverseJacobiCN[v, m]` gives the inverse Jacobi elliptic function cn⁻¹(v|m).

## Examples

Evaluate the inverse Jacobi CN function:

```wolfram
InverseJacobiCN[0.5, 0.3]
(* 1.0472 *)
```

Symbolic evaluation:

```wolfram
InverseJacobiCN[1, m]
(* 0 *)
```
