---
title: DotPlusLayer
---

`DotPlusLayer[n]` is equivalent to LinearLayer[n] and should no longer be used.

## Examples

```wolfram
DotPlusLayer[5]
(* LinearLayer[5] *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/DotPlusLayer.html) for more details.*
