---
title: InverseJacobiSN
---

`InverseJacobiSN[v, m]` gives the inverse Jacobi elliptic function sn⁻¹(v|m).

## Examples

Compute the inverse Jacobi SN:
```wolfram
InverseJacobiSN[0.5, 0.3]
```

Verify the inverse:
```wolfram
JacobiSN[InverseJacobiSN[0.7, 0.5], 0.5]
```
