# Median

`Median[data]` gives the median of the elements in `data`. `Median[dist]` gives the median of the distribution `dist`.

## Examples

Compute the median of a list:

```wolfram
Median[{1, 2, 3, 4, 5}]
(* 3 *)

Median[{1, 2, 3, 4}]
(* 5/2 *)
```
