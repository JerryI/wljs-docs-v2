---
title: DistributionParameterAssumptions
---

`DistributionParameterAssumptions[dist]` gives a logical expression for assumptions on parameters in the symbolic distribution dist.

## Examples

Get parameter assumptions for a normal distribution:

```wolfram
DistributionParameterAssumptions[NormalDistribution[μ, σ]]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/DistributionParameterAssumptions.html) for more details.*
