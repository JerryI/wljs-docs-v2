---
title: ViewAngle
---

`ViewAngle` is an option for `Graphics3D` and related functions that gives the opening angle for a simulated camera used to view the three-dimensional scene.

## Examples

```wolfram
Graphics3D[Sphere[], ViewAngle -> 35 Degree]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/ViewAngle.html) for more details.*
