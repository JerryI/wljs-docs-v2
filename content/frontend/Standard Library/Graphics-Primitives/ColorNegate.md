---
title: ColorNegate
---

`ColorNegate[color]` gives the negative of a color.

`ColorNegate[image]` gives the negative of image, in which all colors have been negated.

## Examples

Negate a color:

```wolfram
ColorNegate[Red]
(* Cyan *)
```

Negate an image:

```wolfram
ColorNegate[img]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/ColorNegate.html) for more details.*
