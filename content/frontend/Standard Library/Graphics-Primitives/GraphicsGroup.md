---
title: GraphicsGroup
---

`GraphicsGroup[{g1, g2, …}]` represents a collection of graphics objects grouped together for purposes of interactive selection in a notebook.

## Examples

```wolfram
Graphics[GraphicsGroup[{Circle[], Rectangle[{0.2, 0.2}, {0.8, 0.8}]}]]
```

```wolfram
Graphics[GraphicsGroup[{Red, Disk[], Blue, Rectangle[]}]]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/GraphicsGroup.html) for more details.*
