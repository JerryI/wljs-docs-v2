---
title: HalfLine
---

`HalfLine[{p1, p2}]` represents the half-line from the point p1 through p2.

- `HalfLine[p, v]` represents the half-line from the point p in the direction v.

## Examples

```wolfram
Graphics[HalfLine[{{0, 0}, {1, 1}}]]
```

```wolfram
Graphics3D[HalfLine[{0, 0, 0}, {1, 1, 1}]]
```
