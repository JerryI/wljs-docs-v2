---
title: NonConstants
---

`NonConstants` is an option for `D` which gives a list of objects to be taken to depend implicitly on the differentiation variables.

## Examples

```wolfram
D[f[x] + g[x], x, NonConstants -> {g}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/NonConstants.html) for more details.*
