---
title: IndependentPhysicalQuantity
---

`IndependentPhysicalQuantity[string]` represents a physical quantity string with no relationship to other physical quantities used in `QuantityVariable`.

## Examples

```wolfram
IndependentPhysicalQuantity["MyCustomQuantity"]
```

```wolfram
QuantityVariable[x, IndependentPhysicalQuantity["Entropy"]]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/IndependentPhysicalQuantity.html) for more details.*
