---
title: SubtitleTrackSelection
---

`SubtitleTrackSelection` is an option that specifies the subtitle tracks of interest.

## Examples

Select specific subtitle tracks:

```wolfram
Video[file, SubtitleTrackSelection -> 1]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/SubtitleTrackSelection.html) for more details.*
