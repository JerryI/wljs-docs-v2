---
title: LibraryUnload
---

`LibraryUnload[lib]` unloads all functions that have been loaded from a Wolfram Library, then it unloads the library.

## Examples

Unload a library:

```wolfram
LibraryUnload["myLibrary"]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/LibraryUnload.html) for more details.*
