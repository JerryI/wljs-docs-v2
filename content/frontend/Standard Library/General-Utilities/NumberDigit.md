---
title: NumberDigit
---

`NumberDigit[x,n]` returns the digit corresponding to 10^n in the real-valued number x.

`NumberDigit[x,n,b]` returns the digit corresponding to b^n.

## Examples

Get the ones digit:

```wolfram
NumberDigit[1234, 0]
(* 4 *)
```

Get the hundreds digit:

```wolfram
NumberDigit[1234, 2]
(* 2 *)
```
