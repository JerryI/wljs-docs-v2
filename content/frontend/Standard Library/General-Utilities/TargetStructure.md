---
title: TargetStructure
---

`TargetStructure` is an option for linear algebra functions that specifies the representation of the result produced by the function.

## Examples

```wolfram
SingularValueDecomposition[{{1, 2}, {3, 4}}, TargetStructure -> "Structured"]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/TargetStructure.html) for more details.*
