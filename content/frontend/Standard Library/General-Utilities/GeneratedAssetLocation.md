---
title: GeneratedAssetLocation
---

`GeneratedAssetLocation` is an option for functions like VideoGenerator that specifies the location of the resulting asset.

## Examples

Generate a video to a specific location:

```wolfram
VideoGenerator[f, 10, GeneratedAssetLocation -> "/path/to/output"]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/GeneratedAssetLocation.html) for more details.*
