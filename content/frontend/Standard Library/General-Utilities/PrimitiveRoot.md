# PrimitiveRoot

`PrimitiveRoot[n]` gives a primitive root of n.

`PrimitiveRoot[n, k]` gives the smallest primitive root of n greater than or equal to k.

## Examples

```wolfram
PrimitiveRoot[7]
(* 3 *)
```

```wolfram
PrimitiveRoot[13, 5]
(* 6 *)
```
