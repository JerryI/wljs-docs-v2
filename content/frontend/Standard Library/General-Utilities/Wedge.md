---
title: Wedge
---

`Wedge[x, y, ...]` displays as x ∧ y ∧ ... and represents the wedge product (exterior product).

## Examples

Wedge product:

```wolfram
Wedge[a, b]
(* a ∧ b *)
```

Anticommutativity:

```wolfram
Wedge[a, b] == -Wedge[b, a]
(* True *)
```

Used in differential forms and exterior algebra.
