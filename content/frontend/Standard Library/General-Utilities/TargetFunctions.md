---
title: TargetFunctions
---

`TargetFunctions` is an option for functions such as `ComplexExpand` and `FindDistribution` that specifies what functions to attempt to generate in the output.

## Examples

```wolfram
ComplexExpand[Abs[a + b I], TargetFunctions -> {Re, Im}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/TargetFunctions.html) for more details.*
