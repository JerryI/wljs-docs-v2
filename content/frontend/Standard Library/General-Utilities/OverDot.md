---
title: OverDot
---

`OverDot[expr]` displays with a dot over expr.

`OverDot[expr,n]` prints with n dots.

## Examples

Display a variable with a dot over it:

```wolfram
OverDot[x]
(* ẋ *)
```

Display with two dots:

```wolfram
OverDot[x, 2]
(* ẍ *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/OverDot.html) for more details.*
