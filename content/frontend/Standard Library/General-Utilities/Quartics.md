---
title: Quartics
---

`Quartics` is an option for functions that involve solving algebraic equations that specifies whether explicit forms for solutions to quartic equations should be given.

## Examples

```wolfram
Solve[x^4 - 1 == 0, x, Quartics -> True]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Quartics.html) for more details.*
