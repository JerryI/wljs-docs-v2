---
title: FractionLine
---

`FractionLine` is an option for fractions that specifies the thickness of the line separating the numerator and denominator.

## Examples

Display a fraction with a thick line:

```wolfram
Style[1/2, FractionLine -> 2]
```

Display a fraction with a thin line:

```wolfram
Style[a/b, FractionLine -> 0.5]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/FractionLine.html) for more details.*
