---
title: Beep
---

`Beep[]` generates an audible beep when evaluated.

`Beep["message"]` beeps and populates the Why the Beep dialog with message.

## Examples

Generate a beep:

```wolfram
Beep[]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Beep.html) for more details.*
