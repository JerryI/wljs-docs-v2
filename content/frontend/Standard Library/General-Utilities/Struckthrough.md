---
title: Struckthrough
---

`Struckthrough` represents a font with a strike-through line.

## Examples

Apply strikethrough formatting to text:

```wolfram
Style["deleted text", FontVariations -> {"Struckthrough" -> True}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Struckthrough.html) for more details.*
