---
title: VariogramFunction
---

`VariogramFunction` is an option to `SpatialEstimate` that specifies the local variation model to use.

## Examples

```wolfram
SpatialEstimate[data, VariogramFunction -> "Spherical"]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/VariogramFunction.html) for more details.*
