---
title: NoWhitespace
---

`NoWhitespace` represents the absence of whitespace between elements in a grammar rules pattern.

## Examples

Match text without whitespace between elements:

```wolfram
GrammarRules[{"ab" ~~ NoWhitespace ~~ "cd"}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/NoWhitespace.html) for more details.*
