---
title: PodStates
---

`PodStates` is an option for `WolframAlpha` that determines information about the states of the pods.

## Examples

```wolfram
WolframAlpha["population of France", PodStates -> {"Result:Show more"}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/PodStates.html) for more details.*
