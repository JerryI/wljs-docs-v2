title: Top
---

`Top` is a symbol that represents the top for purposes of alignment and positioning.

## Examples

Align at top:

```wolfram
Graphics[{
  Rectangle[{0, 0}, {1, 1}],
  Text["Label", {0.5, 1}, {0, -1}]
}]
```

Use in item positioning:

```wolfram
Item["text", Alignment -> Top]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Top.html) for more details.*
