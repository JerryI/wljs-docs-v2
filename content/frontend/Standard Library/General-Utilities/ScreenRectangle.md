---
title: ScreenRectangle
---

`ScreenRectangle` is a global option that specifies the position of the primary screen by giving the coordinates of two diagonally opposite corners.

## Examples

```wolfram
$ScreenRectangle
(* {{0, 1920}, {0, 1080}} *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/ScreenRectangle.html) for more details.*
