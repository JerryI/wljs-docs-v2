---
title: QPolyGamma
---

`QPolyGamma[z, q]` gives the q-digamma function ψ_q(z).

- `QPolyGamma[n, z, q]` gives the nth derivative of the q-digamma function ψ_q^(n)(z).

## Examples

```wolfram
QPolyGamma[2, 0.5]
```

```wolfram
QPolyGamma[1, 3, 0.8]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/QPolyGamma.html) for more details.*
