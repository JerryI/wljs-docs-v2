---
title: IntersectingQ
---

`IntersectingQ[list1, list2]` yields True if list1 and list2 have at least one element in common, and False otherwise.

## Examples

```wolfram
IntersectingQ[{1, 2, 3}, {3, 4, 5}]
```

```wolfram
IntersectingQ[{a, b, c}, {d, e, f}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/IntersectingQ.html) for more details.*
