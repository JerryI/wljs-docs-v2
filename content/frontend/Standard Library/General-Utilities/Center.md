---
title: Center
---

`Center` is a symbol that represents the center for purposes of alignment and positioning.

## Examples

Center text within a framed region:

```wolfram
Framed["Hello", Alignment -> Center]
```

Position a label at the center of an image:

```wolfram
Graphics[{Circle[], Text["Origin", {0, 0}]}, Axes -> True]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Center.html) for more details.*
