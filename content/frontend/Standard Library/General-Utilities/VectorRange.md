---
title: VectorRange
---

`VectorRange` is an option for `VectorPlot` and related functions that specifies the range of vector magnitudes to include in a plot.

## Examples

```wolfram
VectorPlot[{y, -x}, {x, -3, 3}, {y, -3, 3}, VectorRange -> {0.5, 2}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/VectorRange.html) for more details.*
