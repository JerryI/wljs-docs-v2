---
title: ProgressReporting
---

`ProgressReporting` is an option for various algorithmic functions that specifies whether to report the progress of the computation.

## Examples

```wolfram
FindShortestPath[g, start, end, ProgressReporting -> True]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/ProgressReporting.html) for more details.*
