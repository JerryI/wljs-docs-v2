---
title: CoxIngersollRossProcess
---

`CoxIngersollRossProcess[μ, σ, θ, x0]` represents a Cox–Ingersoll–Ross process with long‐term mean μ, volatility σ, speed of adjustment θ, and initial condition x0.

## Examples

Create a CIR process:

```wolfram
CoxIngersollRossProcess[0.05, 0.1, 0.5, 0.03]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/CoxIngersollRossProcess.html) for more details.*
