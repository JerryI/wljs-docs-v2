---
title: ConfidenceRange
---

`ConfidenceRange` is an option for SurvivalModelFit and other functions that specifies the range over which simultaneous confidence intervals and bands are computed.

## Examples

Specify a confidence range for survival model fitting:

```wolfram
SurvivalModelFit[data, ConfidenceRange -> {0, 100}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/ConfidenceRange.html) for more details.*
