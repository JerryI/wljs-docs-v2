---
title: SelfLoopStyle
---

`SelfLoopStyle` is an option for `GraphPlot` and related functions that specifies how to draw self-loops that connect a vertex to itself.

## Examples

```wolfram
Graph[{1 -> 1, 1 -> 2}, SelfLoopStyle -> Red]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/SelfLoopStyle.html) for more details.*
