---
title: ControllableDecomposition
---

`ControllableDecomposition[sys]` yields the controllable subsystem of the state-space model sys.

`ControllableDecomposition[sys, {z1, ...}]` specifies the new state variables zi.

## Examples

Get the controllable subsystem:

```wolfram
ControllableDecomposition[stateSpaceModel]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/ControllableDecomposition.html) for more details.*
