---
title: CombinatorK
---

`CombinatorK` represents the **K** combinator (constant combinator).

## Examples

Apply K combinator:

```wolfram
CombinatorK[x][y]
(* x *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/CombinatorK.html) for more details.*
