---
title: CapitalDifferentialD
---

`CapitalDifferentialD[x]` displays as ⅅx, representing a differential operator.

## Examples

Use in integrals:

```wolfram
Integrate[f[x], CapitalDifferentialD[x]]
```

Symbolic differential:

```wolfram
CapitalDifferentialD[x]
(* ⅅx *)
```
