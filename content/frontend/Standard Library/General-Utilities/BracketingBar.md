---
title: BracketingBar
---

`BracketingBar[x, y, ...]` displays as |x, y, ...|.

## Examples

Display with bracketing bars:

```wolfram
BracketingBar[a, b, c]
(* |a, b, c| *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/BracketingBar.html) for more details.*
