---
title: SequenceForm
---

`SequenceForm[expr1, expr2, ...]` prints as the textual concatenation of the printed forms of the *expri*.

## Examples

```wolfram
SequenceForm["x = ", 5, ", y = ", 10]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/SequenceForm.html) for more details.*
