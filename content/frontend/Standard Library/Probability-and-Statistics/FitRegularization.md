---
title: FitRegularization
---

`FitRegularization` is an option for `Fit` and `FindFit` that specifies a regularization for fitting a model.

## Examples

```wolfram
Fit[data, {1, x, x^2}, x, FitRegularization -> "L2"]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/FitRegularization.html) for more details.*
