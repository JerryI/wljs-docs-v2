---
title: DiscretizeRegion
---

`DiscretizeRegion[reg]` discretizes a region reg into a `MeshRegion`.

`DiscretizeRegion[reg, {{xmin, xmax}, ...}]` restricts to the bounds [xmin, xmax]×....

## Examples

Discretize a disk:

```wolfram
DiscretizeRegion[Disk[]]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/DiscretizeRegion.html) for more details.*
