# RegionProduct

`RegionProduct[reg1, reg2]` represents the Cartesian product of the regions reg1 and reg2.

- `RegionProduct[reg1, reg2, …]` represents the Cartesian product of the regions reg1, reg2, ….

## Examples

```wolfram
RegionProduct[Disk[], Line[{{0}, {1}}]]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/RegionProduct.html) for more details.*
