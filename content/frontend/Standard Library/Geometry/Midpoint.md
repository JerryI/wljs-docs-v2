# Midpoint

`Midpoint[{p1,p2}]` gives the midpoint of the line segment connecting the points p1 and p2.

`Midpoint[Line[{p1,p2}]]` gives the midpoint of a line.

## Examples

```wolfram
Midpoint[{{0, 0}, {2, 4}}]
(* {1, 2} *)
```

```wolfram
Midpoint[Line[{{0, 0}, {10, 10}}]]
(* {5, 5} *)
```
