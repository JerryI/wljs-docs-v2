---
title: PlayRange
---

`PlayRange` is an option for `Play` and related functions which specifies what range of sound amplitude levels should be included.

## Examples

```wolfram
Play[Sin[440 2 Pi t], {t, 0, 1}, PlayRange -> {-0.5, 0.5}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/PlayRange.html) for more details.*
