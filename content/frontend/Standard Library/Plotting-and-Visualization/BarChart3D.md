---
title: BarChart3D
---

`BarChart3D[{y1, y2, ...}]` makes a 3D bar chart with bar lengths y1, y2, ....

`BarChart3D[{data1, data2, ...}]` makes a 3D bar chart from multiple datasets datai.

## Examples

Create a 3D bar chart:

```wolfram
BarChart3D[{1, 3, 2, 5, 4}]
```

Multiple datasets:

```wolfram
BarChart3D[{{1, 2, 3}, {4, 5, 6}}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/BarChart3D.html) for more details.*
