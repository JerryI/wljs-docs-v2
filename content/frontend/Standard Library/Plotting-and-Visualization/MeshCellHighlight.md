---
title: MeshCellHighlight
---

`MeshCellHighlight` is an option and annotation of `MeshRegion`, `BoundaryMeshRegion`, and related functions that specifies mesh cells to highlight.

## Examples

```wolfram
MeshRegion[{{0, 0}, {1, 0}, {1, 1}, {0, 1}}, Polygon[{1, 2, 3, 4}], 
  MeshCellHighlight -> {2 -> Red}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/MeshCellHighlight.html) for more details.*
