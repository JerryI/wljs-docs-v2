---
title: PieChart3D
---

`PieChart3D[{y1, y2, …}]` makes a 3D pie chart with sector angle proportional to y1, y2, ….

## Examples

Basic 3D pie chart:

```wolfram
PieChart3D[{1, 2, 3, 4}]
```

With labels:

```wolfram
PieChart3D[{25, 35, 40}, ChartLabels -> {"A", "B", "C"}]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/PieChart3D.html) for more details.*
