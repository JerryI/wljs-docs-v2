---
title: Axis
---

`Axis` is a symbol that represents the axis for purposes of alignment and positioning.

## Examples

Align elements on the axis:

```wolfram
Graphics[{Circle[], Point[{0, 0}]}, AlignmentPoint -> Axis]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Axis.html) for more details.*
