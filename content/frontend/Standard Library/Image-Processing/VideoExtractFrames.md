---
title: VideoExtractFrames
---

`VideoExtractFrames[video,t]` extracts a frame at time t from video.

`VideoExtractFrames[video,tspec]` extracts video frames at time specification tspec.

## Examples

Extract a frame at 5 seconds:

```wolfram
VideoExtractFrames[video, Quantity[5, "Seconds"]]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/VideoExtractFrames.html) for more details.*
