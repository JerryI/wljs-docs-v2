---
title: GeoAntipode
---

`GeoAntipode[loc]` gives the antipodal position of location `loc`.

- `GeoAntipode[g]` gives the antipodal primitive of the geo primitive `g`.

## Examples

```wolfram
GeoAntipode[Entity["City", {"London", "GreaterLondon", "UnitedKingdom"}]]
```

```wolfram
GeoAntipode[GeoPosition[{40.7, -74.0}]]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/GeoAntipode.html) for more details.*
