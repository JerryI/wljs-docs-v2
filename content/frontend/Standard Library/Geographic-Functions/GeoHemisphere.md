---
title: GeoHemisphere
---

`GeoHemisphere[]` is a two-dimensional `GeoGraphics` primitive that represents the half of the Earth centered at your current geo location.

`GeoHemisphere[loc]` represents the half of the Earth centered at the location loc.

## Examples

```wolfram
GeoGraphics[GeoHemisphere[Entity["City", {"Tokyo", "Tokyo", "Japan"}]]]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/GeoHemisphere.html) for more details.*
