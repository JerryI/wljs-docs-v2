---
title: GeoScaleBar
---

`GeoScaleBar` is an option for `GeoGraphics` that determines what scale to show on the map.

## Examples

```wolfram
GeoGraphics[GeoScaleBar -> "Imperial"]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/GeoScaleBar.html) for more details.*
