---
title: NonPositiveReals
---

`NonPositiveReals` represents the domain of non-positive real numbers.

## Examples

Test membership:

```wolfram
Element[-1.5, NonPositiveReals]
(* True *)
```

Positive fails:

```wolfram
Element[0.5, NonPositiveReals]
(* False *)
```
