---
title: NotSquareSupersetEqual
---

`NotSquareSupersetEqual[x,y,…]` displays as x⋣y⋣….

## Examples

Display a not square superset or equal relation:

```wolfram
NotSquareSupersetEqual[a, b, c]
(* a⋣b⋣c *)
```
