---
title: KnightTourGraph
---

`KnightTourGraph[m, n]` gives a Knight's tour graph on an m×n chessboard.

## Examples

Knight tour graph on a 5×5 board:

```wolfram
KnightTourGraph[5, 5]
```

Smaller board:

```wolfram
KnightTourGraph[3, 4]
```
