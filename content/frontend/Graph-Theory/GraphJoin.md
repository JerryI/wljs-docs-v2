---
title: GraphJoin
---

`GraphJoin[g1, g2]` gives the graph join of the graphs `g1` and `g2`.

## Examples

```wolfram
GraphJoin[CycleGraph[4], CycleGraph[3]]
```

```wolfram
GraphJoin[PathGraph[{1, 2, 3}], PathGraph[{a, b, c}]]
```
