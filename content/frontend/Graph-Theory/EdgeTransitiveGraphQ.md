---
title: EdgeTransitiveGraphQ
---

`EdgeTransitiveGraphQ[g]` yields `True` if the graph g is an edge-transitive graph and `False` otherwise.

## Examples

Check if a graph is edge-transitive:

```wolfram
EdgeTransitiveGraphQ[CompleteGraph[4]]
```
