---
title: EdgeChromaticNumber
---

`EdgeChromaticNumber[g]` gives the chromatic number for the edges of the graph g.

## Examples

Get edge chromatic number of a complete graph:

```wolfram
EdgeChromaticNumber[CompleteGraph[5]]
```
