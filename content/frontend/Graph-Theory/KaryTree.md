---
title: KaryTree
---

`KaryTree[n]` gives a binary tree with `n` vertices.

- `KaryTree[n, k]` gives a k-ary tree with `n` vertices.

## Examples

```wolfram
KaryTree[15]
```

```wolfram
KaryTree[31, 3]
```
