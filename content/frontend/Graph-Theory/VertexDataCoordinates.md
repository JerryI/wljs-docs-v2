---
title: VertexDataCoordinates
---

`VertexDataCoordinates` is an option to `Raster3D` that determines how to map data to the displayed range.

## Examples

Use VertexDataCoordinates with Raster3D:

```wolfram
Raster3D[data, VertexDataCoordinates -> True]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/VertexDataCoordinates.html) for more details.*
