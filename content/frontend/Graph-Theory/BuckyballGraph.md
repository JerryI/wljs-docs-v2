---
title: BuckyballGraph
---

`BuckyballGraph[]` gives the buckyball graph.

`BuckyballGraph[n]` gives the order‐n buckyball graph.

`BuckyballGraph[n,"class"]` gives the order‐n buckyball graph of class "class".

## Examples

Create the standard buckyball graph:

```wolfram
BuckyballGraph[]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/BuckyballGraph.html) for more details.
