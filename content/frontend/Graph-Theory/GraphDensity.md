---
title: GraphDensity
---

`GraphDensity[g]` gives the graph density of the graph `g`.

- `GraphDensity[{v -> w, …}]` uses rules `v -> w` to specify the graph `g`.

## Examples

```wolfram
GraphDensity[CompleteGraph[10]]
```

```wolfram
GraphDensity[RandomGraph[{20, 50}]]
```
