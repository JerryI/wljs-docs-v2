---
title: CayleyGraph
---

`CayleyGraph[group]` returns a Cayley graph representation of a group.

## Examples

Create the Cayley graph of a symmetric group:

```wolfram
CayleyGraph[SymmetricGroup[3]]
(* Graph[...] *)
```

Cayley graph of a dihedral group:

```wolfram
CayleyGraph[DihedralGroup[4]]
(* Graph[...] *)
```
