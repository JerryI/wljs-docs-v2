---
title: ButterflyGraph
---

`ButterflyGraph[n]` gives the order-n butterfly graph.

`ButterflyGraph[n, b]` gives the base-b order-n butterfly graph.

## Examples

Create a butterfly graph:

```wolfram
ButterflyGraph[3]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/ButterflyGraph.html) for more details.*
