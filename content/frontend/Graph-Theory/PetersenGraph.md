---
title: PetersenGraph
---

`PetersenGraph[n, k]` gives the generalized Petersen graph P(n, k).

## Examples

Classic Petersen graph:

```wolfram
PetersenGraph[5, 2]
```

Different parameters:

```wolfram
PetersenGraph[7, 3]
```
