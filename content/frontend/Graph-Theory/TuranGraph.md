---
title: TuranGraph
---

`TuranGraph[n, k]` gives the k-partite Turán graph with n vertices T(n,k).

## Examples

Create a Turán graph:

```wolfram
TuranGraph[6, 3]
```

Visualize:

```wolfram
Graph[TuranGraph[8, 4]]
```

Edge count:

```wolfram
EdgeCount[TuranGraph[10, 5]]
```
