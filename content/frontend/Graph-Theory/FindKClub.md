---
title: FindKClub
---

`FindKClub[g,k]` finds a largest k-club in the graph g.

## Examples

```wolfram
(* Find a 2-club *)
g = RandomGraph[{10, 20}];
FindKClub[g, 2]

(* Find a 3-club *)
FindKClub[g, 3]
```
