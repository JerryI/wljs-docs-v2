---
title: EdgeCycleMatrix
---

`EdgeCycleMatrix[g]` gives the edge cycle matrix of a graph g.

## Examples

Get edge cycle matrix:

```wolfram
EdgeCycleMatrix[CycleGraph[4]]
```

For a complete graph:

```wolfram
EdgeCycleMatrix[CompleteGraph[4]]
```
