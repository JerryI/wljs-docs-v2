---
title: GraphUnion
---

`GraphUnion[g1,g2]` gives the graph union of the graphs g1 and g2.

`GraphUnion[g1,g2,…]` gives the graph union of g1, g2, ….

`GraphUnion[{v->w,…},…]` uses rules v->w to specify the graph g.

## Examples

```wolfram
GraphUnion[CycleGraph[3], CycleGraph[4]]
```
