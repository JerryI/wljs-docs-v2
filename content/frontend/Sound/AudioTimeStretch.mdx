---
title: AudioTimeStretch
---

`AudioTimeStretch[audio,r]` applies time stretching to audio by the specified factor r.

`AudioTimeStretch[video,r]` applies time stretching to the first audio track in video.

## Examples

Stretch audio to twice its duration:

```wolfram
AudioTimeStretch[audio, 2]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/AudioTimeStretch.html) for more details.
