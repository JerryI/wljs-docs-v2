```wolfram
Play[f, {t, tmin, tmax}, opts___] _Sound
```

creates an object that plays as a sound whose amplitude is given by `f` as a function of time `t`.

## Options

### `SampleRate`
The default is `8000`

<Callout type="note">
Is effectively generate [SampledSoundList](../Sound/SampledSoundList) and feed it to [Sound](../Sound/Sound)
</Callout>
