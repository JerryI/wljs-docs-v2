---
title: MusicTimeSignature
package: wljs-sound
---

```wolfram
MusicTimeSignature[numerator]
MusicTimeSignature[numerator, denominator]
MusicTimeSignature[numerator, denominator, beatLength]
MusicTimeSignature[properties]
```

represents the meter of a musical container.

```wolfram
MusicTimeSignature[4, 4]
MusicTimeSignature[6, 8]
```

The available properties are `"Numerator"`, `"Denominator"` and `"BeatLength"`.

```wolfram
signature = MusicTimeSignature[6, 8];
signature["BeatLength"]
```

Attach a time signature through a container property:

```wolfram
MusicMeasure[
  {"G4", "A4", "B4"},
  <|"TimeSignature" -> MusicTimeSignature[3, 4]|>
]
```

Different measures in one [MusicVoice](../Sound/MusicVoice) may use different time signatures.

See the [Wolfram Language reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/MusicTimeSignature.html) for simple and compound meter behavior.
