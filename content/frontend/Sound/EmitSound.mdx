```wolfram
EmitSound[_Audio | _Sound, opts___]
```

emits (without output or any widgets) a [Sound](../Sound/Sound) or plays [Audio](../Sound/Audio) object. 

## Options
### `"Window"`
The same as in [FrontSubmit](../Frontend-IO/FrontSubmit). By the default is [CurrentWindow](../Frontend-IO/CurrentWindow)

<Callout type="tip">
If you want to continuously stream raw sound data, consider to use a low-level streamer [PCMPlayer](../Sound/PCMPlayer)
</Callout>
