---
title: AudioReverse
---

`AudioReverse[audio]` reverses audio so that the signal is played backward.

`AudioReverse[video]` reverses the first audio track in video.

## Examples

Reverse audio playback:

```wolfram
audio = ExampleData[{"Audio", "Bird"}];
AudioReverse[audio]
```
