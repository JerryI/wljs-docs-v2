---
title: AudioSplit
---

`AudioSplit[audio, t]` splits audio at time t.

`AudioSplit[audio, {t1, t2, ...}]` splits audio at times ti.

## Examples

Split audio at 2 seconds:

```wolfram
audio = ExampleData[{"Audio", "Bird"}];
AudioSplit[audio, 2]
(* {Audio[...], Audio[...]} *)
```
