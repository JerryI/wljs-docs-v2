---
title: ContourIntegrate
---

`ContourIntegrate[f, z ∈ cont]` gives the integral of f along the contour defined by cont in the complex plane.

## Examples

Integrate along a circle contour:

```wolfram
ContourIntegrate[1/z, z ∈ Circle[0, 1]]
(* 2 π I *)
```
