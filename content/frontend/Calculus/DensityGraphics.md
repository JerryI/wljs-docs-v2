---
title: DensityGraphics
---

`DensityGraphics[array]` is a representation of a density plot.

## Examples

Create density graphics:

```wolfram
DensityGraphics[Table[Sin[x] Cos[y], {x, 0, 2Pi, 0.1}, {y, 0, 2Pi, 0.1}]]
```
