---
title: Decompose
---

`Decompose[poly, x]` decomposes a polynomial, if possible, into a composition of simpler polynomials.

## Examples

Decompose a polynomial:

```wolfram
Decompose[x^4 + 2x^2 + 1, x]
(* {1 + x^2, x^2} *)
```
