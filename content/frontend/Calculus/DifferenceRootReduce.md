---
title: DifferenceRootReduce
---

`DifferenceRootReduce[expr,n]` attempts to reduce expr to a single DifferenceRoot object as a function of n.

## Examples

```wolfram
DifferenceRootReduce[Fibonacci[n], n]
(* DifferenceRoot[...] *)
```
