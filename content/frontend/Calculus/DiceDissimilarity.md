---
title: DiceDissimilarity
---

`DiceDissimilarity[x,y]` gives the Dice dissimilarity between Boolean vectors x and y.

## Examples

```wolfram
DiceDissimilarity[{1, 0, 1, 1}, {0, 1, 1, 0}]
```
