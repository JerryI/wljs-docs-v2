---
title: Limit
---

`Limit[f, x -> x0]` gives the limit of f as x approaches x0.

## Examples

Limit at a point:

```wolfram
Limit[Sin[x]/x, x -> 0]
(* 1 *)
```

Limit at infinity:

```wolfram
Limit[1/x, x -> Infinity]
(* 0 *)
```
