---
title: DiagonalizableMatrixQ
---

`DiagonalizableMatrixQ[m]` gives True if m is diagonalizable, and False otherwise.

## Examples

```wolfram
DiagonalizableMatrixQ[{{1, 2}, {0, 3}}]
(* True *)
```

```wolfram
DiagonalizableMatrixQ[{{1, 1}, {0, 1}}]
(* False *)
```
