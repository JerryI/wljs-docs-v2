---
title: ToSphericalCoordinates
---

`ToSphericalCoordinates[{x, y, z}]` gives the {r, θ, ϕ} spherical coordinates corresponding to the Cartesian coordinates {x, y, z}.

## Examples

```wolfram
ToSphericalCoordinates[{1, 1, 1}]
```

```wolfram
ToSphericalCoordinates[{0, 0, 5}]
```
