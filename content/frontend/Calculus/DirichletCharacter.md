---
title: DirichletCharacter
---

`DirichletCharacter[k, j, n]` gives the Dirichlet character χk,j(n) with modulus k and index j.

## Examples

Compute a Dirichlet character:

```wolfram
DirichletCharacter[5, 2, 3]
```
