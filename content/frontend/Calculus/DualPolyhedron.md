---
title: DualPolyhedron
---

`DualPolyhedron[poly]` gives the dual polyhedron of the polyhedron poly.

## Examples

Get the dual of a cube (octahedron):

```wolfram
DualPolyhedron[PolyhedronData["Cube", "Polyhedron"]]
```
