---
title: DiscreteHadamardTransform
---

`DiscreteHadamardTransform[list]` gives the discrete Hadamard transform of list.

## Examples

Compute the discrete Hadamard transform of a list:

```wolfram
DiscreteHadamardTransform[{1, 2, 3, 4}]
(* {5, -1, -2, 0} *)
```
