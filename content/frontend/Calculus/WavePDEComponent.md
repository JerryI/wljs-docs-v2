---
title: WavePDEComponent
---

`WavePDEComponent[vars, pars]` yields a wave equation PDE term ∂²u/∂t² - c²∇²u with model variables vars and model parameters pars.

## Examples

```wolfram
WavePDEComponent[{u[t, x, y], t, {x, y}}, {{"WaveSpeed" -> 1}}]
```
