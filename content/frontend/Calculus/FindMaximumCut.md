---
title: FindMaximumCut
---

`FindMaximumCut[g]` gives the maximum cut of the graph g.

## Examples

Find the maximum cut of a graph:

```wolfram
FindMaximumCut[CycleGraph[5]]
(* {{1, 3}, {2, 4, 5}} *)
```
