---
title: Curl
---

`Curl[{f1, f2, f3}, {x1, x2, x3}]` gives the curl of a 3D vector field.

## Examples

Curl of a vector field:

```wolfram
Curl[{y, -x, 0}, {x, y, z}]
(* {0, 0, -2} *)
```

2D curl (scalar):

```wolfram
Curl[{-y, x}, {x, y}]
(* 2 *)
```
