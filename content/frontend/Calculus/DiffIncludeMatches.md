---
title: DiffIncludeMatches
---

`DiffIncludeMatches` is an option for `Diff` and related functions that specifies whether to include matching data in the resulting `DiffObject`.

## Examples

Include matches in diff:

```wolfram
Diff["abc", "aXc", DiffIncludeMatches -> True]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/DiffIncludeMatches.html) for more details.*
