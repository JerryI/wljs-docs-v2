---
title: Area
---

`Area[reg]` gives the area of a two-dimensional region.

## Examples

Area of a disk:

```wolfram
Area[Disk[{0, 0}, r]]
(* Pi r^2 *)
```

Area of a polygon:

```wolfram
Area[Polygon[{{0, 0}, {1, 0}, {1, 1}, {0, 1}}]]
(* 1 *)
```
