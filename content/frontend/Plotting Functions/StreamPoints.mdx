---
title: StreamPoints
---

`StreamPoints` is an option to `StreamPlot`, `ListStreamPlot`, and related functions that determines how many streamlines to draw.

## Examples

```wolfram
StreamPlot[{y, -x}, {x, -2, 2}, {y, -2, 2}, StreamPoints -> 20]
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/StreamPoints.html) for more details.*
