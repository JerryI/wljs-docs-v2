---
title: Plot3D
---

`Plot3D[f, {x, xmin, xmax}, {y, ymin, ymax}]` generates a three-dimensional plot of f as a function of x and y.

## Examples

Plot a 3D surface:

```wolfram
Plot3D[Sin[x] Cos[y], {x, -Pi, Pi}, {y, -Pi, Pi}]
```

With options:

```wolfram
Plot3D[x^2 + y^2, {x, -2, 2}, {y, -2, 2}, ColorFunction -> "Rainbow"]
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Plot3D.html) for more details.
