---
title: SpokenString
---

`SpokenString[expr]` gives a string of text corresponding to a spoken representation of the expression expr.

## Examples

Get a spoken representation of an expression:

```wolfram
SpokenString[x^2 + 1]
(* "x squared plus 1" *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/SpokenString.html) for more details.*
