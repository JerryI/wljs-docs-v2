---
title: StringRotateRight
---

`StringRotateRight[string, n]` cycles the characters in *string* *n* positions to the right.

- `StringRotateRight[string]` cycles one position to the right.

## Examples

```wolfram
StringRotateRight["abcdef", 2]
(* "efabcd" *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/StringRotateRight.html) for more details.
