---
title: Alphabet
---

`Alphabet[]` gives a list of the lowercase letters a through z in the English alphabet.

`Alphabet[type]` gives the alphabet for the specified language or class.

## Examples

English alphabet:

```wolfram
Alphabet[]
(* {"a", "b", "c", ..., "z"} *)
```

Greek alphabet:

```wolfram
Alphabet["Greek"]
(* {"α", "β", "γ", ..., "ω"} *)
```
