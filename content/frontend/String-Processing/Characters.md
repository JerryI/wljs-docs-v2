---
title: Characters
---

`Characters["string"]` gives a list of the characters in a string.

## Examples

Split into characters:

```wolfram
Characters["Hello"]
(* {"H", "e", "l", "l", "o"} *)
```

Count characters:

```wolfram
Length[Characters["example"]]
(* 7 *)
```
