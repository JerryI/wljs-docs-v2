---
title: RemoveDiacritics
---

`RemoveDiacritics[string]` replaces characters in string that have diacritics by their base ASCII characters, when possible.

## Examples

Remove diacritics from text:

```wolfram
RemoveDiacritics["café"]
(* "cafe" *)
```
