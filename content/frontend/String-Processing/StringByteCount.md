---
title: StringByteCount
---

`StringByteCount["string"]` gives the total number of bytes used to store the characters in a string.

## Examples

```wolfram
StringByteCount["Hello"]
(* 5 *)
```

```wolfram
StringByteCount["日本語"]
(* 9 *)
```
