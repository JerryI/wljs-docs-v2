```wolfram
CopyToClipboard[expr_, opts___]
```

pushes [InputForm](../Formatting/InputForm) form of `expr` to the clipboard of a user.

## Options
In a case of a lost context provide 

### `"Window"`
Specify explicitly a notebook where to pop up a window. By the default is [CurrentWindow](../Frontend-IO/CurrentWindow)
