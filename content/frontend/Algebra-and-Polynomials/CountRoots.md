---
title: CountRoots
---

`CountRoots[f, x]` gives the number of real roots of the univariate function f in x.

`CountRoots[f, {x, a, b}]` gives the number of roots between a and b.

## Examples

Count roots of a polynomial:

```wolfram
CountRoots[x^3 - x, x]
(* 3 *)
```
