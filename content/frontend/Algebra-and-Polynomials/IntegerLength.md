---
title: IntegerLength
---

`IntegerLength[n]` gives the number of digits in the base 10 representation of n.

`IntegerLength[n, b]` gives the number of digits in base b.

## Examples

Decimal digits:

```wolfram
IntegerLength[12345]
(* 5 *)
```

Binary digits:

```wolfram
IntegerLength[255, 2]
(* 8 *)
```
