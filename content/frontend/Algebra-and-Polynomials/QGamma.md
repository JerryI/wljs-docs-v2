---
title: QGamma
---

`QGamma[z, q]` gives the q-gamma function Γq(z).

## Examples

Evaluate q-gamma:

```wolfram
QGamma[3, 0.5]
```

Symbolic form:

```wolfram
QGamma[n, q]
```

Limit as q→1:

```wolfram
Limit[QGamma[3, q], q -> 1]
(* 2 (= Gamma[3] = 2!) *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/QGamma.html) for more details.*
