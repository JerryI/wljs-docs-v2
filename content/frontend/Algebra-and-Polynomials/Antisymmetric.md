---
title: Antisymmetric
---

`Antisymmetric[{s1, ..., sn}]` represents the symmetry of a tensor that is antisymmetric in the slots si.

## Examples

Define an antisymmetric tensor:

```wolfram
TensorSymmetry[LeviCivitaTensor[3]]
(* Antisymmetric[{1, 2, 3}] *)
```
