---
title: ThompsonGroupTh
---

`ThompsonGroupTh[]` represents the sporadic simple Thompson group Th.

## Examples

Create the Thompson group:
```wolfram
ThompsonGroupTh[]
```

Get group order:
```wolfram
GroupOrder[ThompsonGroupTh[]]
```
