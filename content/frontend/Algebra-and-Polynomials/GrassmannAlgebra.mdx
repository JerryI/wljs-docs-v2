---
title: GrassmannAlgebra
---

```wolfram
GrassmannAlgebra[vars]
GrassmannAlgebra[vars, alg]
```

constructs a [NonCommutativeAlgebra](../Algebra-and-Polynomials/NonCommutativeAlgebra) representing the Grassmann, or exterior, algebra with generators `vars`.

## Example

```wolfram
alg = GrassmannAlgebra[{x, y, z}];
NonCommutativeExpand[
  Wedge[x + 2 y + 3 z, 4 x + 5 y + 6 z],
  alg
]
```

The generators anticommute and their squares vanish.

See the official [Wolfram Language reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/GrassmannAlgebra.html) for the canonical representation and supported operations.
