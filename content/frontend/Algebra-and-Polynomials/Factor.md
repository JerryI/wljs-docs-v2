---
title: Factor
---

`Factor[poly]` factors a polynomial over the integers.

## Examples

Factor a polynomial:

```wolfram
Factor[x^2 - 1]
(* (x - 1)(x + 1) *)
```

Factor a cubic:

```wolfram
Factor[x^3 - 1]
(* (x - 1)(x^2 + x + 1) *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/Factor.html) for more details.
