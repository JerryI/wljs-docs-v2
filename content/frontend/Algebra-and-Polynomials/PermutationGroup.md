---
title: PermutationGroup
---

`PermutationGroup[{perm1, …, permn}]` represents the group generated by multiplication of the permutations perm1, …, permn.

## Examples

```wolfram
PermutationGroup[{Cycles[{{1, 2}}], Cycles[{{2, 3}}]}]
```
