title: JankoGroupJ4
---

`JankoGroupJ4[]` represents the sporadic simple Janko group J4.

## Examples

Get the order of the Janko group J4:

```wolfram
GroupOrder[JankoGroupJ4[]]
(* 86775571046077562880 *)
```
