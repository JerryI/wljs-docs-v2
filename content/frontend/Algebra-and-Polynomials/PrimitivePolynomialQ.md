---
title: PrimitivePolynomialQ
---

`PrimitivePolynomialQ[poly, p]` tests whether poly is a primitive polynomial modulo a prime p.

## Examples

```wolfram
PrimitivePolynomialQ[x^4 + x + 1, 2]
```

```wolfram
PrimitivePolynomialQ[x^2 + 1, 3]
```
