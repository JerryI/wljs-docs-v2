---
title: CliffordAlgebra
---

```wolfram
CliffordAlgebra[{pvars, nvars, zvars}]
CliffordAlgebra[{pvars, nvars, zvars}, alg]
```

constructs a [NonCommutativeAlgebra](../Algebra-and-Polynomials/NonCommutativeAlgebra) representing a generalized Clifford algebra. Generators in `pvars`, `nvars` and `zvars` square to `1`, `-1` and `0`, respectively.

## Example

```wolfram
alg = CliffordAlgebra[{{e1, e2}, {e3}, {}}];
NonCommutativeExpand[(e1 + e3) ** (e1 - e3), alg]
```

The second form takes operation names and monomial-order settings from `alg`.

See the official [Wolfram Language reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/CliffordAlgebra.html) for the defining relations.
