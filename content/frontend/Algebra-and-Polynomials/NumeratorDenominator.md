---
title: NumeratorDenominator
---

`NumeratorDenominator[expr]` gives the list `{Numerator[expr], Denominator[expr]}` of expr.

## Examples

```wolfram
NumeratorDenominator[3/4]
```

```wolfram
NumeratorDenominator[(a + b)/(c + d)]
```
