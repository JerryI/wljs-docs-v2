---
title: DihedralGroup
---

`DihedralGroup[n]` represents the dihedral group of order 2n.

## Examples

Create the dihedral group of a triangle:

```wolfram
DihedralGroup[3]
(* DihedralGroup[3] *)
```

Get group elements:

```wolfram
GroupElements[DihedralGroup[4]]
```

Group order:

```wolfram
GroupOrder[DihedralGroup[5]]
(* 10 *)
```
