---
title: Antihermitian
---

`Antihermitian[{1, 2}]` represents the symmetry of an antihermitian matrix.

## Examples

Define a tensor with antihermitian symmetry:

```wolfram
TensorSymmetry[{{0, I}, {-I, 0}}]
(* Antihermitian[{1, 2}] *)
```
