---
title: WeylAlgebra
---

```wolfram
WeylAlgebra[{vars, dvars}]
WeylAlgebra[{vars, dvars}, alg]
```

constructs a [NonCommutativeAlgebra](../Algebra-and-Polynomials/NonCommutativeAlgebra) representing the Weyl algebra with variables `vars` and corresponding derivative generators `dvars`.

## Example

```wolfram
alg = WeylAlgebra[{{x, y}, {dx, dy}}];
NonCommutativeExpand[dy ** dx ** x ** y, alg]
```

The second form takes operation names and monomial-order settings from `alg`.

See the official [Wolfram Language reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/WeylAlgebra.html) for the defining commutation relations.
