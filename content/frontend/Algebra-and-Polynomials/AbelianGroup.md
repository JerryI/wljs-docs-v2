---
title: AbelianGroup
---

`AbelianGroup[{n1, n2, ...}]` represents the direct product of the cyclic groups of degrees n1, n2, ....

## Example

Define an abelian group and get its elements:

```wolfram
g = AbelianGroup[{2, 3}];
GroupElements[g]
(* {{0, 0}, {0, 1}, {0, 2}, {1, 0}, {1, 1}, {1, 2}} *)
```
