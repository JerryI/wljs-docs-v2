---
title: LyonsGroupLy
---

`LyonsGroupLy[]` represents the sporadic simple Lyons group Ly.

## Examples

Get the order of the Lyons group:

```wolfram
GroupOrder[LyonsGroupLy[]]
(* 51765179004000000 *)
```
