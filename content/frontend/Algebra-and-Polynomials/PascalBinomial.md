---
title: PascalBinomial
---

`PascalBinomial[n, m]` gives the binomial coefficient C(n, m) that preserves Pascal's identity.

## Examples

```wolfram
PascalBinomial[5, 2]
```

```wolfram
Table[PascalBinomial[n, k], {n, 0, 5}, {k, 0, n}]
```
