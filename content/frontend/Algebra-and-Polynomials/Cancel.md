---
title: Cancel
---

`Cancel[expr]` cancels out common factors in the numerator and denominator of expr.

## Examples

Cancel common factors:

```wolfram
Cancel[(x^2 - 1)/(x - 1)]
(* x + 1 *)
```

```wolfram
Cancel[(x^2 + 2x + 1)/(x + 1)]
(* x + 1 *)
```
