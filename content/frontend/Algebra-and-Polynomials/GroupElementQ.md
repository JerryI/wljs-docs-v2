---
title: GroupElementQ
---

`GroupElementQ[group, g]` returns `True` if the object `g` is an element of `group` and `False` otherwise.

## Examples

```wolfram
GroupElementQ[SymmetricGroup[4], Cycles[{{1, 2, 3}}]]
```

```wolfram
GroupElementQ[AlternatingGroup[4], Cycles[{{1, 2}}]]
```
