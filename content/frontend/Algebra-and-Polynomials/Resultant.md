---
title: Resultant
---

`Resultant[poly1, poly2, var]` computes the resultant of two polynomials with respect to var.

## Examples

Compute resultant:

```wolfram
Resultant[x^2 - 1, x^2 - 4, x]
(* 9 *)
```

Resultant of linear polynomials:

```wolfram
Resultant[a*x + b, c*x + d, x]
(* a d - b c *)
```
