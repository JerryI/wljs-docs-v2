---
title: PermutationOrder
---

`PermutationOrder[perm]` gives the order of permutation perm.

## Examples

Find the order of a permutation:
```wolfram
PermutationOrder[Cycles[{{1, 2, 3}}]]
```

Order of a transposition:
```wolfram
PermutationOrder[Cycles[{{1, 2}}]]
```
