---
title: NumberFieldRootsOfUnity
---

`NumberFieldRootsOfUnity[a]` gives the roots of unity for the field ℚ[a] generated by the algebraic number a.

## Examples

```wolfram
NumberFieldRootsOfUnity[Sqrt[5]]
(* {2, -1} *)
```

```wolfram
NumberFieldRootsOfUnity[Exp[2 Pi I/6]]
(* {6, E^(I Pi/3)} *)
```
