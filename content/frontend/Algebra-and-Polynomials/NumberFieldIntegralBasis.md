---
title: NumberFieldIntegralBasis
---

`NumberFieldIntegralBasis[a]` gives an integral basis for the field ℚ[a] generated by the algebraic number a.

## Examples

```wolfram
NumberFieldIntegralBasis[Sqrt[5]]
```
