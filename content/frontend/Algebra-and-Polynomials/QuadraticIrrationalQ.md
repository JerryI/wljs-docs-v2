---
title: QuadraticIrrationalQ
---

`QuadraticIrrationalQ[x]` gives `True` if x is a quadratic irrational and `False` otherwise.

## Examples

```wolfram
QuadraticIrrationalQ[Sqrt[2]]
(* True *)
```
