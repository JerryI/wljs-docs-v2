title: Complex
---

`Complex` is the head used for complex numbers.

## Examples

Create a complex number:

```wolfram
Complex[3, 4]
(* 3 + 4 I *)
```

Check the head of a complex number:

```wolfram
Head[2 + 3 I]
(* Complex *)
```
