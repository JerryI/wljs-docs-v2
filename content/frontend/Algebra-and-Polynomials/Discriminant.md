---
title: Discriminant
---

`Discriminant[poly, var]` computes the discriminant of the polynomial poly with respect to var.

## Examples

Discriminant of a quadratic:

```wolfram
Discriminant[a*x^2 + b*x + c, x]
(* b^2 - 4 a c *)
```

Numeric example:

```wolfram
Discriminant[x^2 - 5x + 6, x]
(* 1 *)
```
