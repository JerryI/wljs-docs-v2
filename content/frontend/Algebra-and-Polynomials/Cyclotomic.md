---
title: Cyclotomic
---

`Cyclotomic[n, x]` gives the nth cyclotomic polynomial in x.

## Examples

First few cyclotomic polynomials:

```wolfram
Cyclotomic[1, x]
(* x - 1 *)

Cyclotomic[2, x]
(* x + 1 *)

Cyclotomic[6, x]
(* x^2 - x + 1 *)
```
