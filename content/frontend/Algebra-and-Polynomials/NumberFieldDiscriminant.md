---
title: NumberFieldDiscriminant
---

`NumberFieldDiscriminant[a]` gives the discriminant of the field ℚ[a] generated by the algebraic number a.

## Examples

```wolfram
NumberFieldDiscriminant[Sqrt[5]]
(* 5 *)
```

```wolfram
NumberFieldDiscriminant[2^(1/3)]
(* -108 *)
```
