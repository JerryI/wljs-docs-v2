---
title: PermutationProduct
---

`PermutationProduct[a, b, c]` gives the product of permutations a, b, c.

## Examples

```wolfram
PermutationProduct[Cycles[{{1, 2}}], Cycles[{{2, 3}}]]
```

```wolfram
PermutationProduct[{2, 3, 1}, {3, 1, 2}]
```
