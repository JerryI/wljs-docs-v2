title: MonsterGroupM
---

`MonsterGroupM[]` represents the sporadic simple monster group M.

## Examples

Get the order of the monster group:

```wolfram
GroupOrder[MonsterGroupM[]]
(* 808017424794512875886459904961710757005754368000000000 *)
```
