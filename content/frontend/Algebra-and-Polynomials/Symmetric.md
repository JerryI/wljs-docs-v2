---
title: Symmetric
---

`Symmetric[{s1, ..., sn}]` represents the symmetry of a tensor that is symmetric in the slots *si*.

## Examples

```wolfram
TensorSymmetry[{{a, b}, {b, c}}]
(* Symmetric[{1, 2}] *)
```
