title: Rational
---

`Rational` is the head used for rational numbers.

## Examples

Check head of a fraction:

```wolfram
Head[3/4]
(* Rational *)
```

Pattern matching:

```wolfram
MatchQ[5/7, _Rational]
(* True *)
```
