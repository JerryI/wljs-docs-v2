---
title: AlternatingGroup
---

`AlternatingGroup[n]` represents the alternating group of degree n.

## Examples

```wolfram
AlternatingGroup[4]
(* AlternatingGroup[4] *)
```

```wolfram
GroupOrder[AlternatingGroup[5]]
(* 60 *)
```
