---
title: QBinomial
---

`QBinomial[n, m, q]` gives the q-binomial coefficient (Gaussian binomial).

## Examples

Compute a q-binomial coefficient:
```wolfram
QBinomial[5, 2, q]
```

Evaluate at a specific q value:
```wolfram
QBinomial[4, 2, 2]
```
