---
title: NonCommutativeExpand
---

```wolfram
NonCommutativeExpand[expr]
NonCommutativeExpand[expr, alg]
```

expands non-commutative products in `expr`. With a second argument, multiplication, addition and algebra relations are taken from `alg`.

## Example

```wolfram
NonCommutativeExpand[(a + 2 b) ** (3 a + 4 b)]
```

Unlike ordinary `Expand`, the order of factors joined by `**` is preserved.

See the official [Wolfram Language reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/NonCommutativeExpand.html) for more details.
