---
title: BitNot
---

`BitNot[n]` gives the bitwise NOT of the integer n.

## Examples

Bitwise NOT:

```wolfram
BitNot[5]
(* -6 *)
```

```wolfram
BitNot[0]
(* -1 *)
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/BitNot.html) for more details.*
