---
title: PutAppend
---

`expr>>>filename` appends expr to a file.

`PutAppend[expr1,expr2,…,"filename"]` appends a sequence of expressions expri to a file.

## Examples

```wolfram
{1, 2, 3} >>> "data.txt"
```

*Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/PutAppend.html) for more details.*
