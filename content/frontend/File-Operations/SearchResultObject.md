---
title: SearchResultObject
---

`SearchResultObject[...]` represents a result from `TextSearch[...]` and related functions.

## Examples

```wolfram
res = TextSearch[index, "query"];
res["Snippet"]
```

```wolfram
SearchResultObject[...]["Location"]
```
