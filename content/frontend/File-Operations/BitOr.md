---
title: BitOr
---

`BitOr[n1, n2, ...]` gives the bitwise OR of the integers ni.

## Examples

Bitwise OR of two integers:

```wolfram
BitOr[5, 3]
(* 7 *)
```

Multiple operands:

```wolfram
BitOr[1, 2, 4]
(* 7 *)
```

Please visit the official [Wolfram Language Reference](https://reference.wolfram.com/language/ref/BitOr.html) for more details.
