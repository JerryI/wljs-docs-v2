---
title: RenameFile
---

`RenameFile[file1, file2]` renames or moves file1 to file2.

## Examples

Rename a file:

```wolfram
RenameFile["old.txt", "new.txt"]
(* "new.txt" *)
```

Move to different directory:

```wolfram
RenameFile["data.csv", "archive/data.csv"]
```
