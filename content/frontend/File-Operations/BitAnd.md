---
title: BitAnd
---

`BitAnd[n1, n2, ...]` gives the bitwise AND of the integers ni.

## Examples

Bitwise AND of two integers:

```wolfram
BitAnd[5, 3]
(* 1 *)
```

Multiple operands:

```wolfram
BitAnd[15, 7, 3]
(* 3 *)
```
